The testing and learning that takes place during Kaggle competitions is invaluable to the broader data science community. We’d like to invest more in helping participants distill and promote these insights widely.

For submitting a winner’s write up for the Feedback Prize competition (using this template &amp; documentation), we'd like to offer,



The top 50 teams (either leaderboard or efficiency prize): Kaggle-branded swag (t-shirt or water bottle) for every team member

The top 10 teams (either leaderboard or efficiency prize): $1,000/team, in addition to swag for every team member.


This pilot will help us learn what content is most helpful to the ML community and begin testing the best ways to promote this knowledge to developers beyond Kaggle.

How to participate:



After the competition closes, publicly publish a winner’s write up using this template as a discussion post in the competition forum by Monday, December 19 at 11:59 PM UTC.

Once published, fill out this short form to let us know it’s complete.

We’ll then email you a confirmation and mail over the gift.


Please reach out to me if you have any questions or would like to share feedback on how we could make this better at mark@kaggle.com.

Best of luck!

Mark
