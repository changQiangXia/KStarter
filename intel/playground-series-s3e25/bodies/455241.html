Domain elements and reading materials

  1. https://www.nps.gov/articles/mohs-hardness-scale.htm -- defines the Mohs hardness scale
  2. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohs_scale -- wiki-article
  3. https://www.gemsociety.org/article/select-gems-ordered-mohs-hardness/ -- very good starter article
  4. https://geology.com/minerals/mohs-hardness-scale.shtml -- well written explanatory article on the Mohs scale
  5. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZUgWzRpA-4 -- well explained YouTube video
  6. http://www.minsocam.org/msa/collectors_corner/article/mohs.htm -- excellent article for the Mohs hardness scale with some adjutant information
  7. https://www.rocksandminerals.com/hardness/mohs.htm -- article to illustrate how to test a rock/ mineral for its hardness check

  4. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GsnT2X4u_U -- YouTube video to explain the metric in detail
