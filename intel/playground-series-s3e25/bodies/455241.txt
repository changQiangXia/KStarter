Domain elements and reading materials



https://www.nps.gov/articles/mohs-hardness-scale.htm -- defines the Mohs hardness scale

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohs_scale -- wiki-article

https://www.gemsociety.org/article/select-gems-ordered-mohs-hardness/ -- very good starter article 

https://geology.com/minerals/mohs-hardness-scale.shtml -- well written explanatory article on the Mohs scale

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZUgWzRpA-4 -- well explained YouTube video

http://www.minsocam.org/msa/collectors_corner/article/mohs.htm -- excellent article for the Mohs hardness scale with some adjutant information

https://www.rocksandminerals.com/hardness/mohs.htm -- article to illustrate how to test a rock/ mineral for its hardness check


Competition metric - Median Absolute Error



https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_absolute_deviation -- wikipedia article

http://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.median_absolute_error.html -- scikit-learn implementation for the metric

https://www.avmetrics.net/AVM_glossary/median-absolute-error/ -- well explained article with interpretation

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GsnT2X4u_U -- YouTube video to explain the metric in detail
