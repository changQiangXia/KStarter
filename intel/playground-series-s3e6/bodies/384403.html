Small word of caution

  1. The regression challenge is evaluated on the RMSE metric that is highly sensitive to outliers. From most of the EDA and discussion posts as on date, I infer that the training data contains a few outliers that could distort the RMSE and perhaps the CV score. Kindly exercise caution while performing feature engineering, model training and inference development.
  2. The dataset is quite small and could be noisy, hence, it will be good to avoid extremely baroque approaches. Also, from the past 5 episodes, I observe that the public leaderboard correlates fairly lowly with the private leaderboard and there has been a significant churn. Hence, developing models that well and truly generalize could be a good option too.
