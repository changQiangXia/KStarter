Overview

Features created using train.csv, tracking.csv, bbox.csv and pretrained models (yolov7, mmpose) for the video data is input to xgboost along with predictions of the cnn part. CV with csv data only is 0.766, CV with pretrained models added is 0.774, and 0.7955 with the CNN part added.

CNN Part

2.5d CNN is used for contact prediction. In this part, @tanakar in especially made a great contribution.

Input Image

Grayscale images are cropped around the helmet's bbox and resized to 224x224.
Inputs are different for player &amp; player contacts and player &amp; ground contacts.



player &amp; player


crop range up to 2 times the rectangle surrounding the two bboxes

input bbox mask as an additional channel

±2step

player &amp; ground


crop 6x around the bbox

no mask

±2step

player &amp; player


use samples with predictions of 1st stage lightgbm greater than 0.01

player &amp; ground

The simple average of multiple backbones is used as the features of next xgb. 

Table &amp; Pretrained Models Part

Player Detection

Player was detected using pretrained YOLOv7 and detected boxes were tracked using REPP, and matched with helmets. We inferred in steps instead of frames to speed up the submission time. In the matching process, bboxes were extracted in the order of the longest tracked length and greedily matched to the helmets of the players that matched best. The features created are as follows



size of the bbox

aspect ratio of the bbox

distance between the center coordinates of the two players' bboxes (dx, dy, dist, dist/width)

IoU of the bbox of the two players

area of overlap with the bbox of the helmet

tracked length

matching score (sum of overlapped area)



Pose Estimation

We used mmpose to estimate the players' posture. mmpose saved inference time by using the players' bboxes that were object detected by yolov7. In addition, inference was performed only once in 2steps because this part was not significant for the computation time.



distance between head and foot, hand and foot

knee, hip angle

number of keypoints detected and sum of confiences

distance between head and center of helmet bbox


Coordinates Gap Between Field and Camera View

tracking.csv does not include a vertical component. Optimize the homographic transformation from field coordinates to camera coordinates and calculate the displacement of the helmet position. This misalignment can be attributed to the misalignment between the measured position of the field coordinates and the position of the helmet, and the vertical coordinates of the player, from which we can extract the vertical component. This idea is based on this solution by @its7171 from the last year's solution. The homography transformation was optimized using cv2.findHomography.



Coordinates (x, y) after projection

Number of players used to optimize the homography matrix

Distance between the coordinates after projection and the center coordinates of the helmet (dx, dy, dist, dx/width, dy/width dist/width)

Total distance between the post-projection coordinates and the center coordinates of the helmet in frames



Other Features



step, max(step), step / max(step)

amount of movement from the start/end of the match (dx, dy, dist)

distance to the nth nearest player (n=1, 2, 5, 10, 15)

distance from the center of gravity coordinates of all players (dx, dy, dist)

difference, product, and sum of velocity, acceleration, distance, and angle of two players

aspect ratio of the bboxes of the helmets

distance between the center coordinates of the bboxes of the two helmets

IoU of the tow helmets

position

same team or not (0/1)

lag features for the most important features among the above (diff, shift, lag=±1, 2, 5, 10, 20)


Other Tips



To make the experiment more efficient, we trained lightgbm, which predicts contact faster with fewer features, and excluded samples that were obviously unnecessary from penguin&amp;tk pipeline.

xgboost was about 0.002~4 better than lightgbm.

When using cutoff with qishen&amp;bo's predictions, add a mask feature to shows it has been cutoff.


Not Worked



LSTM, Transformer, 1DCNN

data augmentation by swapping nfl_player_id_1, nfl_player_id_2 in table part

instance segmentation

seed averaging improved by less than 0.001
