(I'm writing this similar topic for all current tiered competitions.)

TL;DR

Before team merge, check carefully if the person you are merging with is trustworthy. Do not base your merging decision solely on the fact that he is a master or grandmaster. They may have obtained their Master or Grand Master status by cheating. Also, once a cheater steals your answers, they can be misused for various secondary cheating activities.

This is the scheme how cheaters steals your solutions:
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/otto-recommender-system/discussion/381527

Happy Kaggling!

Detail information

The scheme shared above link is a kind of social engineering where network of cheaters shares their teammates code/submission file privately. I don’t know this competitions submission policy, however, I think this kind of cheating is appreciable to any kind of competitions unless the host checks duplication/similarity of the solutions.

So, I believe the only way to avoid this is participants watch very carefully to not to share their code to suspicious persons.

This scheme was reported by multiple teams on the on-going Otto’s competition, and some accounts are suspected as cheaters.
