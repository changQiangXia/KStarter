Metric tip

This metric is also used in the House Prices - Advanced Regression Techniques starter competition.
One may convert the target to it's logarithm and proceed with the RMSE metric to replicate the RMSLE. I suggest this is an easier way to handle the metric rather than designing a custom GBM objective/ metric evaluation function.
