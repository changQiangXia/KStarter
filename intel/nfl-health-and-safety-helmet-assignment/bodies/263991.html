Some of the common things that worked across the solutions:

2 stages learning where first stage locates where helmets might impact (generally a 2D detection model) and second stage does the classification on more frames (generally a 3D classification model).
EfficientDet or YOLO are often used as object detection models
Many solutions used only images and videos and some used tracking data.
