Concern: I think this year, there was too much noise not just in Kaggle Notebooks but in Discussions. I was unable to share anything with the Kaggle Competition community and unable to make my solutions public. I planned to make my solution public a month ago, but each day I saw tens of new notebooks and some random discussion posts. 😑

Let me share my solution. I'll keep it simple.

My personal GPU rig got fried a couple of weeks before the competition, and I only had access to a single small personal GPU at home. I didn't want to go through the hassle of setting the rig up again. Google's Perchv2 got a lot of attention, and I initially thought to finetune that at the start of the competition, but I couldn't fit it in my GPU. Later, I borrowed a GPU from a friend, but by then I couldn't plan to finetune Perch.

I know a lot of people train models with small Mel spectrograms because they contain enough information, but I think small models like EfficientNet-B0 are not as perfect as they should be considering their receptive field. To help, I like the idea of increasing Mels. Even though it increases compute, I couldn't afford to pre-train a better architecture with small Mels.
@cooolz did the same in previous competitions, so I followed that approach. Ultimately, I saw that increasing Mels beyond 480 didn't help, so I stopped there.

Melspec Settings



Mels: 480

Hop Length: 1001

N_FFT: 4096


The reason for the 1001 hop length is that for 20 seconds of audio, I wanted to get exactly 20 frame probabilities as outputs, basically a single framewise probability for each second.
