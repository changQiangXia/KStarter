3. Trainable perchv2

The pytorch model is convert from tf version with the help of gpt. And there is a public availabel one produced by hengck23

4. Train params

Cos scheduel with warmup from 0 to 2.5e-4 to 1e-6 with around 10 epochs. Bce loss

5. Sample strategy,

80-500, if a label has morethan 500 files, give a low sample weight. if a label has less than 80 files, give a high sample weight. striong soundscape data x2.5 weight

6. augmantation,

  • 0.8 probability: random gain ±4 dB
  • 0.5 probability: random time and frequency masking
  • 1.0 probability: mixup

7. Inference & Post-processing

The final inference uses ONNX-exported model via OpenVINO. Post-processing pipeline:

60s audio → Mel Spectrogram → Single Model → Prior Fusion
    → Smooth Day/Night → Sigmoid
    → Amphibia Month Prior → File Confidence Scale
    → Per-Taxon Temporal Smoothing → Final Probs

7.1. Inference

  • 5-fold ONNX models
  • Input: 60s audio → 6000-frame mel spec → model → 12×5s clip logits
  • Output: logits (12, 234)

7.2. post process

7.2.1. Prior Fusion

  • Site, hour, site×hour priors fitted from soundscape strong labels

7.2.2. Smooth Day/Night Correction

Many species follow unique day-night activity rhythms, analogous to higher cold drink sales in summer. Their hour-wise priors are computed from probability distributions of soundscape labels; supporting evidence is shown below.

7.2.3. Amphibia Month Prior

Amphibian activity fluctuates between dry and wet seasons, similar to seasonal beverage consumption trends. Monthly priors are fitted from soundscape probability statistics, with relevant evidence attached below.

This echoes a well-known ML anecdote: a DL expert fitted beverage store data and concluded cold drinks sell more in summer and hot drinks sell better in winter.
