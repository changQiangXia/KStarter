Below is the train data time series for breath_id = 28. It has parameters R=20 and C=20. What do these parameters mean? (Explanation part 1 of 2 is below. And part 2 of 2 is here)

What is u_in and pressure?

The comp data is time series. In the plot above, the x-axis represents time. Imagine that we have a lung which can be thought of as a balloon. At time t, we blow air into the balloon (lung) in a quantity indicated by the blue line (u_in). At time t, the pressure inside the balloon (lung) is indicated by the orange line ('pressure). Time to left of dotted black line has balloon's exit closed (inhale), and time to right of dotted black line has balloon's exit open (exhale).

What Does C Mean?

To understand parameter C, we can use RAPIDS Nearest Neighbors (KNN) and RAPIDS KMeans to find 300 additional time series in the train data with the same u_in but different C parameter. We plot all 300 of these time series (on same axis) below. (Kaggle notebook here)

The blue lines are all the u_in time series. Notice how they are all similar. The red lines are the pressures of all time series with C=10. The orange lines are pressures with C=20. And the yellow lines are pressures with C=50.

The variable C is compliance and indicates how easy it is to stretch the balloon (lung). The largest value C=50 is the easiest to stretch. If we input air into a balloon that stretches easily, the pressure does not increase because the balloon just gets bigger (and thus air per space does not increase). If we input air into a ballon that does not stretch easy (C=10), the balloon stays the same size but gets more air inside (thus pressure increases).

What Does R Mean?

The variable R is resistance and indicates how easy it is for air to enter and then "exit" the balloon. (Actually just move around since during inhale exit is closed). The largest value R=50 has the most resistance. If we input air into a balloon with high resistance, the air "stays inside" and increases pressure.
