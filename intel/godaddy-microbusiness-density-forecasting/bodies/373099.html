Competition Summary

In Kaggle's GoDaddy Microbusiness Density Forecasting competition, we are given 3135 time series of length 39. These time series are 39 month observations from 3135 unique USA counties between the months of August 2019 thru October 2022 inclusive. We are asked to predict microbusiness density for the next 8 months which are November 2022 thru June 2023 inclusive. Microbusiness density is the number of microbusinesses per 100 adults in specific county.

Here is one of the 3135 time series for county numbered 21087. The first two numbers 21 indicate Kentucky and the last three numbers 087 indicate county Green County. The microbusiness density is around 1 and the unit for Kaggle train data is "per 100 adults". The train data also gives us active = 80. So we know that Kentucky's Green County has population 8000 adults = (100 * active) / microdensity (in 2020 since census data is lagged by 2 years)

Competition Metric

We are asked to make 25080 predictions = 3135 * 8 and the public LB is the evaluation of 3135 predictions for the most recent month using SMAPE metric. The private LB is the evaluation of predictions after current month.

Local Validation

Locally we can compute validation score by using the first 38 months of Kaggle train as "new train" and the last 1 month as "new public leaderboard LB". Or if we want to evaluate private LB, we can use first 35 months as "new train", next 1 month as "public LB" and next 3 months as "private LB". Kaggle has said here that the final private leaderboard will be 3 months of data with a 1 month gap between train data and test data. Notebook to compute local validation (for public LB) is here

Importance of Population

Note that some counties have small population. And it is very difficult to predict the future microbusiness activity when population is small because we have both fewer history observations and the future will be significantly affected by a few events.

For example Loving County Texas has population 90 adults with microbusiness density of 1. The future of Loving County Texas will change significantly if just 1 adult begins or stops microbusiness activity. Therefore in this competition, it is easiest to predict "last value" model for counties with small population and use your model to predict counties with larger population. Because patterns observed in larger populations are more likely to continue into the future. Note the "linear regression" baseline only uses the linear model when population > 25_000 which is roughly the top 50% of counties by size.
