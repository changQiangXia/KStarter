***After this post i received a positive Comments from best kaggle users, and i want to share some of they points, that could make you a bit more effective in this platform.

BEST POINTS:

1). Ignore the medals and upvotes and just enjoy the journey!
2). Always remaind yourself why started on kaggle, if you social life negatively effected take a break
3). Spend a fixed time on kaggle, like 1 hour per day
4). Join only one competition at a time, from start to finish
5). Ignore the public leaderboard and Optimize your cv score
6). Keep a bullet list of lessons learnt. (more on learning and less on leaderboard score)
7). Always remind yourself that it's only a game
8). Better time spent on kaggle than playing video games and wasting your time
9). Better time spend on constantly learning, sharing code, exchanging thoughts, competing then on gambling/drinking/insert-self-destructive-behavior
10). Do some physical exercises (Activity.. walking.. running.. dancing )
