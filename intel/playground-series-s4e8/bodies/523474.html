This dataset has a complex structure, making it challenging to apply standard methods for feature importance, such as Mutual Information (MI). Additionally, most of the features are categorical, which complicates the use of correlation analysis.

We also have these 9 features with a significant number of missing rows:

Enter something that I learned about from the Orange Book of Machine Learning (check it out if you haven't already) by @carlmcbrideellis: Predictive Power Score! The PPS finds every relationship that the correlation finds — and more. You can use the PPS to identify strong predictors for your target column. It is also compatible with categorical data, making it ideal for this dataset. Check out this article for a full explanation of PPS.
