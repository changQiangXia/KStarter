My solution

As I haven't joined any competition in the past few years, and I've only been doing MLOPS at work. I admit that I was "outdated" and by joining, I was "forced" to learn.

I got introduced to the following libraries

  • OPTUNA
  • AUTOGLUON

One of my first solutions was this one 🍄 Model Ensemble Majority Voting + CV, it performed decent at 0.984xx, but looking at the discussion boards, AUTOGLUON was almost always being discussed and used.

I haven't used AUTOGLUON before, so I've spent a few days installing the libraries and setting up my environment on my machine (Intel 13700K + RTX4090). Afterwards I was able to start conducting experiments and used the same feature engineering techniques that I used in the notebook above, and let AUTOGLUON do the crunching.

Eventually, I learned more and more, this is on top of the members that were also sharing their ideas, and incorporated these ideas with my own. I also incorporated the original mushroom dataset into the training data and noticed that it gave me a small boost (after deduplicating of course!)

Training AUTOGLUON models was very tedious and time-consuming. I also had to study, learn, and upskill in the middle of the competition. This is of course, on top of me doing my 9-5 work and other responsibilities. My submission was an ensemble (using MODE) of the 9 highest AUTOGLUON models that I trained in my local machine, with each AUTOGLUON experiment running for 6,12,24,30,36 hours, and with each new experiment, incorporating new ideas that I prototype using non AUTOGLUON means (OPTUNA + CV + manual ensembling)

This submission was 0.98530 in the public score, and was 0.98509 in the private score
