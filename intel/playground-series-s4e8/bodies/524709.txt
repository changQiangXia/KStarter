Introduction

Another Auto GP month has finished and I am able to stay consistent with my third top 3 finish in AutoML Grand Prix. The competition was quite an unique experience where I tried hundreds of Feature Engineering techniques, yet barely anything improved my CV scores. Also my top scoring 3rd place submission (0.98525) includes simple 3 fold ensemble - based on slight modifications in Autogluon baseline shared by @motono0223. For EDA and parameter tuning purpose, I used DriverLessAI for most work. While my other high scorings submissions were a blend of multiple models from H2O DriverLessAI &amp; Neural Network which couldn't beat 0.98525 Leaderboard score in first 24 hours :_) I will try to cover both approaches in this discussion. 

Validation Split: KFold w/ 4 Splits

Winning submission:

0.35 - 0.35 - 0.3 weightage in ensemble across 3 folds (4 fold KFold), yeah it was that easy to be in top 10 finish for first 24 hours or maybe I just got lucky seed :) 
I have published the training code,hyperparameter tweaks and final submission csv on the github repo: https://github.com/Nischaydnk/automl-aug-3rdplace

As suggested by AmbrosM, having good neural networks could be a key thing in this competition, since they are performing almost as good as GBM models. I spent quite a lot of time during the first day on Neural Networks and was able to get 0.98490 CV with Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) blocks. You can find their python implementation here: https://github.com/KindXiaoming/pykan &amp; faster/simple codebase here: https://github.com/ZiyaoLi/fast-kan

Since it wasn't part of my best submission, I haven't shared the notebook yet, but I am planning to publish couple of notebook in the competition with KAN implementation when I find time. I personally find KANs quite effective in finetuning when working with smaller scale networks.

Synthetic Data Generation

I generated several million samples using codebase which is publicly shared to get more augmented data. To test these samples, I trained my models on just generated data and kept the original competition data for validation. I couldn't cross the 0.980 margin and when concatenating with original training dataset, results of combined data was worse than using just competition data. I think the reason could the additional noise introduced in competition's dataset by the hosts.
