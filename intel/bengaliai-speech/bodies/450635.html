Dataset: We directly used the data provided by the organizer and did not use any external data.

Training Environment: One Colab Pro+ account is utilized. Due to limitations of equipment and computing power, we are unable to train more models(like the punctuation model) and complete data sets.

Data Augmentation: Some augmentations like HighLowPass, Noise, and PitchShift are used to increase robustness.

Model Training: Pretrained model is from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/takanashihumbert/bengali-ex002. We put in one-tenth of the data for training each time (feature encoder and feature extractor take turns to freeze). For freezing the feature extractor, the lr is 2e-5 for warmup. For the feature encoder, it is 6e-6. In our experiments, bs=1. After training three-fifths of the data, the public score is 0.42 and private score is 0.503.

Decoding Parameters: We adjust the decoding parameters and displayed them in the notebook https://www.kaggle.com/code/yuliknormanowen/bengali-sr-wav2vec-v1-bengali-inference-for-v4?scriptVersionId=146374376.
