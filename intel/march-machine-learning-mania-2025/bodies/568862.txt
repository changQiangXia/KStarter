If you'd like to visualize your Kaggle submission file as a bracket, you can do so at this webpage:
https://wncviz.com/NCAABrackets/KaggleBrackets.html

When you first navigate to the page, it will display a blank bracket. If you upload your submission, Games that have been scheduled or played will automagically appear. Games that have been played will be shaded according to your prediction. You can hover of the games to get more information and you can also project to see how the tournament might play out according to your predictions.

There are several example submissions accessible via a dropdown menu, if you just want to see how the process works. They date back to 2010 and are all generated via a simple linear regression against an eigenvector based rating system. The most recent one is my 2025 entry which uses linear regression against that same rating system and some other team stats. I doubt it will do particularly well, but maybe it's a sensible baseline.  :/

Evidently, your prediction file need not be recent. In principle, the predictions can be for any tournament for which Kaggle provides data. Your submission need not be complete, either. You can view just a men's or women's submission, for example, and you need only include teams that are actually in the tournament.
