It was important to recognize that CustomerId and Surname are high-cardinality categorical variables and encode them correctly.

Catboost was enough to win this competition. My final submission was based on one Catboost model averaged across 20 folds.

If you tuned Catboost parameters well you would be in Top 3. There're a lot of parameters to tune and there are many ways to encode categorical features. I now realize that my 1st place solution can be improved even further.
