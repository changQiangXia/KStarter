Hello everyone, I have been participating in this competition for almost a week now. This week, I have conducted a lot of experiments, from the initial confusion about how to calculate CV to the subsequent parameter tuning, training models, and so on.

Here, I would like to share some of my experimental records. I find some experimental results very strange, and I hope everyone can communicate together!

My first  baseline model is unet+efficientnet-b0, with a score of around 610 for both cv and lb, and all the following experiments were conducted based on this.




ALL data augmentation do not works for me. It's vert strange. I have tried simple flipping, rotating, and other operations, as well as advanced methods such as mixup, which can cause a decrease in my CV and LB


I tried to fuse images from multiple times to create a 2.5D image, but cv droped…


I replaced efficientnet with mit, but the cv was even worse. eficientnet seems to performe very well in this competition


I simply tried models such as Segformer and Deeplabv3, the results were even worse. but, I did not perform detailed parameter tuning on them. This competition is like those medical image segmentation tasks in Kaggle, where simple unet can achieve good results…


A larger backbone can bring great improvements, similarly, a larger resolution can also bring great improvements. With a larger backbone and a larger resolution, my baseline can be improved to 650+


6.The improvement that pseudo labels  is also very significant. I simply tried some methods of pseudo labels and the trained model has a score of nearly 670



Post processing is very important, and an appropriate threshold generally increases  2% -3%. And I think maybe we can consider implementing this paragraph in the post processing , but currently I don't have a very suitable idea



  
Some key labeling guidance:
  Contrails must contain at least 10 pixels
  At some time in their life, Contrails must be at least 3x longer than they are wide
  Contrails must either appear suddenly or enter from the sides of the image
  Contrails should be visible in at least two image





I have tried many losses, bce and dice achieve the best results, but the combination of the two has not improved😟


Adding auxiliary classification header, failed


As the experiment progresses, my CV seems to be somewhat inconsistent with LB…
