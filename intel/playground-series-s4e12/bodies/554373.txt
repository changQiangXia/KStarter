6th Place Solution Summary

I’d like to share the key points of my 6th place solution, hoping it might help others in future challenges.



1. Feature Engineering with Bin-Classified Predictions and RMSE-Optimized CatBoost

To enhance the predictive features, I adopted two approaches:




Bin-Classified Predictions:  
Although this was a regression task, I divided the target variable into bins (20-400-5000) and trained a CatBoostClassifier to predict these categories. The resulting probabilities were added as new features. Treating all features as categorical data in this model further improved accuracy.


RMSE-Optimized CatBoost Predictions:  
I was deeply inspired by the excellent notebook Rid-Catboost-Nonlog-as-Feature, which showcased a creative and highly effective way to use predictions from a CatBoostRegressor optimized for RMSE as additional features. Following this idea, I trained the regressor on a feature-engineered version of the dataset and used its predictions as new features. This adjustment resulted in significant improvements compared to using predictions from the raw data.


These engineered features played a crucial role in boosting the performance of subsequent models.

2. Stacking with LGBMRegressor and XGBRegressor

After generating the enhanced features, I calculated OOF (Out-Of-Fold) predictions using LGBMRegressor and XGBRegressor. For these models, the CV scores were approximately 1.026 with TabNet and 1.024 with LGBM. These predictions, combined with the bin-classified probabilities and RMSE-optimized CatBoost predictions, were used as inputs for stacking. 

The final submission, which stacked predictions from LGBMRegressor, XGBoostRegressor achieved a score in the 1.023 range on the Private LB. Without stacking, my Private LB score was approximately 1.02412.

3. What I Didn’t Do



I initially considered using the original competition dataset as part of the training data, as I’ve done in previous competitions. However, due to differences in the distribution of the target variable, this approach negatively affected performance, so I decided against it.
