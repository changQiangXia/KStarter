Thanks to the organizers for a very interesting competition, my teammates ( @yasushiyui and @kotaiizuka ) for effort, and all the kagglers who worked hard together.  And special thanks to @its7171, the author of great baseline notebooks.

There are already many great top solutions out there, but we would like to share ours as an example of how just a few tweaks to the baseline can increase your score.Please feel free to comment if you notice anything or would like to know more details.

Summary



Basically, we used the baseline notebook processing pipeline.

Similar to the Public 1st place solution, three neighboring frames were converted to grayscale and combined in the channel direction for using temporal information. We believe that this idea was probably crucial for our solution to enter the public gold medal zone.

We applied the techniques usually used in competitions (high resolution images, modification of loss function, custom data augmentation, ensemble, TTA, etc.).


Using three neighboring frames

The figure below shows the flow of this technique. To avail temporal information and to use the same processing pipeline as for RGB image input, three adjacent images were converted to grayscale and combined in the channel direction. Most experiments used time_stride=1. In one of the final submissions, we ensembled the models trained with time_stride=1, 2, and 3, respectively, to take advantage of the longer temporal information.

data augmentation


RandomAffine, RandomPerspective, RandomHorizontalFlip, ColorJitter(brightness, contrast), Mixup

Averaging checkpoints


default settings for avg_checkpoints.py in timm

Inference


interpolation method for resize: cv2.INTER_AREA

crop_pct: 0.875 (default setting for tf_efficientnet_b0_ap)

3-models ensemble with horizontal flip tta

inference once every 2 frames for speedup (predictions for frames not inferred are generated by linear interpolation)

custom data loader to prefetch images behind the GPU inference process

parameter tunings for window_size and ignore_width in make_sub function
