Having looked at the features interaction, I venture to assume that V20 and V23 are the money features (V20 might be a card balance or avg day spending or amount pending). 

Let's plot the Amount vs V20 and respective correlation:


Now let's divide V20 by Amount:


It seems the fraudsters shoot for the available card limit right away. 

Inverse Correlation emerges when the fraud happens for V27 vs V28:


Having gathered these facts, I decided to see if these features alone work well during the modeling (suppose others are the noise). So I kept only them and added some derivatives. It ended up with the LB score ~0.8. Good luck!

train[] = train.V20 / train.Amount
train[] = train.V23 / train.Amount
train[] = train.V27 / train.V28
train[] = train[] / train[]
train[] = train[] / train[]

test[] = test.V20 / test.Amount
test[] = test.V23 / test.Amount
test[] = test.V27 / test.V28
test[] = test[] / test[]
test[] = test[] / test[]
