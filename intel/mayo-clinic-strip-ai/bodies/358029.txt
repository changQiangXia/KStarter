Thanks everyone for the competition, congratz to the winners &amp; kudos to the learners. 

Although this one is a controversial one because most people couldn't even beat the sample submission, I still believe it was an alright competition. The key is to understand that there is not a lot of signal, and that the competition metric is not friendly with overconfident models, more on that later.

Because of the nature of the competition (not a lot of signal, and basically no public LB), I decided not too invest too much time on it, so I am very happy with the result !

Data



Color normalization : 


Detect the background color

Normalize all the image by  𝑟 = 𝑏𝑎𝑐𝑘𝑔𝑟𝑜𝑢𝑛𝑑_𝑐𝑜𝑙𝑜𝑟/(255,   255,  255)

Simple Approach : 


Remove all white chunks from the image 

Resize to 1024x1024

Advanced Approach (cf figure below): 


Detect duplicated areas in the image

Keep only one of them

Resize to have the longest edge to 1024, preserving aspect ratio




You can see some examples here.

Models



3 Small Models  -  CV AUC 0.684


Resnet10t – AUC 0.661

EfficientNet-b0 – AUC 0.671

EfficientNet-b0 using simple approach images - AUC 0.662

Training


Image size : 1024x1024

Ranger + lr=5e-4 (a, b)   - Adam + lr = 1e-4 (c)

Class-balanced BCE to mimic the metric + Label smoothing

10 epochs, bs=16

Flip &amp; color augmentations

Inference


4 flips TTA

Simple average

Scaling !


Adapting to the Competition Metric





The logloss heavily penalizes confident mistakes, comparatively to the reward for confident guesses


Since our models have low AUCs, we want to avoid the “Heavy Penalty” zone

My ensemble already outputs most of its scores in the “safe” range possibly because  my models were designed to underfit (Label smoothing, small model size, short training with low LRs)


Ensemble CV : 0.655

You can force your model to be safer ! (cf this post)


Linearly Rescale to the 0.15 / 0.85 range

Clip to 0.25 / 0.75

Final CV : 0.640 - Public LB 0.733 - Private LB 0.666


Thanks for reading :)
