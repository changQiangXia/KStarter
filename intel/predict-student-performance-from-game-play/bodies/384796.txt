Game Description

The Jo Wilder game for this competition is a hidden object/quiz game with some text prompts along the way. Main objective of the game is to solve some mysteries and answer quizzes that arise in every chapter (total of 5 chapters). 

Note : I will discuss answers of the quiz questions and discuss the game plot in detail which might ruin the experience of your gameplay. I recommend playing the game at least once to get an idea and then use this discussion thread as a reference. The game can be played online here
