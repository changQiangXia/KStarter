Happy start-of-real-tournaments day! This thread will be the consolidated place where we post scoring updates this year. For those who are unfamiliar, here's how scoring works:

  • New this year, we're pulling game results from the natstat.com API rather than creating a solution file by hand. There can be a few hours delay in the results being available by the API, but hopefully doing this programmatically will eliminate transcription errors.

  • Once submissions close we will manually invalidate the submissions you do not select to count towards scoring. Don't panic that all but two of your submissions are suddenly invalidated.

  • I still have to kick off scoring updates by hand. I'll try to do these as often as possible, but please remember I'm just a regular person and this competition is a labor of love alongside a life and a day job. Why isn't this automated after 9 years of running this competition? It's a long story of priorities and startups and API refactors and oh yeah, the labor of love thing. We'll get there one day…

  • I'll post solution files as forum attachments here so that you can double check accuracy and do your own analysis if you wish. Frequency-wise, expect updates each day that games occur (or potentially the day after, if late games don't update right away). Again, I'll do my best to update more often than once daily, but please don't DM me at 2AM asking why the game that ended at 1AM isn't posted 😅.

Select your submissions and good luck all!
