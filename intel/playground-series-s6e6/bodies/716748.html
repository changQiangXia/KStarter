  • 1 linear target-encoded model: logistic regression.

Upgrade by Codex

To improve this starter notebook, I asked Codex to read my starter (stacker and 19 level 1 models) and suggest upgrades. It gave me a list and I asked Codex to implement its top 2 suggestions. We changed one model and added one model. For the TabICLv2 model, we updated it to finetuning (instead of zero shot). And then we created a one-vs-rest CatBoost model. Therefore my final submission was 20 models.
