Final Submission

My final submission is my public starter notebook that I published during the first week here 😀 (with discussions here, here, here) and then Codex added one model and changed one model. I figured this starter was already near the CV LB ceiling so I didn't spend too much time improving it.

Upgrade by Codex

To improve this starter notebook, I asked Codex to read my starter (stacker and 19 level 1 models) and suggest upgrades.  It gave me a list and I asked Codex to implement its top 2 suggestions. We changed one model and added one model. For the TabICLv2 model, we updated it to finetuning (instead of zero shot). And then we created a one-vs-rest CatBoost model. Therefore my final submission was 20 models.



Starter notebook: CV 0.97028, Public LB 0.97101, Private LB 0.97024 (rank 45th)

Upgraded notebook: CV 0.97045, Public LB 0.97094, Private LB 0.97033 (rank 25th)
