Hi all. I published a starter pack to help everyone in Kaggle's June Playground competition.

This competition uses metric balanced accuracy so a great meta model stacker is GPU accelerated multinomial Logistic Regression. I create a starter notebook here (This is the stacker I used to win 2nd place in April playground here)

For each of your models, just save the OOF and Test PREDS. Also when building your level 1 models, there is no need for post process or any kind of calibration. Just give the raw predictions to Logistic Regression stacker, it will ensemble your models and calibrate at the same time.

I also publish a RealMLP starter here and XGBoost starter here. Both were created by Codex GPT5.5. Enjoy!
