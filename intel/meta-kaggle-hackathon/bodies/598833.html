Main Track

First Place

Echoes of Interest: Finding Similarity in Kaggle Datasets by Daniel Hernández Mota and Diego Alfonso Meza Corona

This writeup uses Meta Kaggle data to show how the community adds a layer of semantic richness on top of Kaggle Datasets through interactions like voting that can help to power personalized recommenders and improve the platform. It’s a fantastic use of the Meta Kaggle dataset and presents really useful suggestions and ideas in a compelling and fun way. Overall, this project was a very strong end-to-end analysis and proof of concept.

Congratulations Daniel & Diego!

Second Place

Why Do So Many Kaggle Users Go Quiet, and What Might Bring Them Back? by Parul Pandey

Parul’s writeup explores not just the growth of Kaggle’s community, but patterns of engagement and churn. Her analysis ties together great insights about Kaggle’s platform, community, and history with highly relevant data from Meta Kaggle to identify important trends and make well-supported recommendations for how to reactivate users. It’s amazing to see how valuable, engaging events like 5 Days of GenAI brought so many community members back to Kaggle and indeed we hope to do more.

Congratulations, Parul!

Third Place

Discussion Activity & Competition Success on Kaggle by Peter Moorhouse

Peter’s writeup explores the significance of open collaboration in competition forums. The analysis is very principled and arrives at some solid conclusions that hopeful competitors should take note of. Importantly, top competitors are shown to openly share their insights with the community during the course of a competition, but still manage to maintain their position on the leaderboard. This open sharing of knowledge as a tide that lifts all boats speaks to what makes Kaggle and its community such a special place.

Congratulations Peter!

Trends Over Time Track

First Place

From Insights to Impact by Alle Deli, 익명의페페로니, Grace “GRACE” Amazing, HyeonYu, and soohee

This writeup is a tour-de-force that dives deep into the Meta Kaggle datasets to create a holistic picture of the state of the Kaggle platform and community, how it’s evolved to where it is today, and challenges that threaten future growth. The authors give a series of recommendations that our team will definitely be looking closely at! Specifically, they use data and great insights to identify opportunities to make Kaggle easier for newcomers to get started and incentives that motivate everyone.

Congratulations Alle Deli, 익명의페페로니, Grace “GRACE” Amazing, HyeonYu, and soohee!

Second Place

Kaggle Chronicles: 15 Years of Competitions, Community & Data Science Innovation by BwandoWando and TheItCrow (Kevin & Brando — Team Bierbingka)

This Writeup chronicles broad-ranging trends across Kaggle community growth, use of AI/ML technology, and competition dynamics. There’s so much richness to explore in their submission, but I’d particularly call out the use of anomaly detection to tie spikes in Kaggle’s user base growth to key events. They’ve also done a lot of interesting work to look at patterns in library usage in competitions and how they fit into larger technological shifts.

Congratulations Brando & Kevin!

Third Place

Kaggle Journeys: Cohorts and Competition Shifts by Steven Sio

Steven’s writeup takes a close look at trends over time in competition participation by doing a cohort analysis and examining differences in submission patterns. There are some super interesting takeaways; for example, how there’s a growing trend of faster-paced, solo participation. Suggestions to cater to more casual and fast-paced competitors by launching shorter, time-bound challenges or mini-competitions are really cool ideas Kaggle should consider.

Congratulations Steven!
