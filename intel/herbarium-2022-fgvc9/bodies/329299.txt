Other settings:
Batch size is always 512 (256 for big models), trained with 100epochs.
torch.amp is adopted.
Following solutions， we adopt similar Post-Process and obtain 0.001 improvement.
We use Swin-B pretrained on Imagenet22k for initialization, for other backbones, we try to use Imagenet22k pretrained weights if possible.
We freeze partial layers to enlarge the batchsize during the training process, We then reduce the batch size and train the full parameters.
