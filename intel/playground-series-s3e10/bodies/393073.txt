Some public notebooks in this competition show confusion matrices and roc curves. In a logloss competition, these metrics aren't helpful. In a logloss competition, you need to look at the calibration plot, and perhaps at a histogram of the predictions:



plt.figure(figsize=(10, 4))
ax = plt.subplot(1, 2, 1) # calibration
plt.title('Calibration')
CalibrationDisplay.from_predictions(train[target], oof, n_bins=100, strategy='quantile', ax=ax)
plt.subplot(1, 2, 2) # histogram
plt.title('Histogram')
plt.hist(oof, bins=100)
plt.show()
