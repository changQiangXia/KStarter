This GitHub repository has year wise collection of research papers: 
https://github.com/zhjohnchan/awesome-image-captioning 

some recent papers are:



Image Captioning with Compositional Neural Module Networks - Tian J et al, IJCAI 2019.

Improving Image Captioning with Better Use of Caption - Shi Z et al, ACL 2020.

Comprehensive Image Captioning via Scene Graph Decomposition - Zhong Y et al, ECCV 2020.

CapWAP: Image Captioning with a Purpose - Fisch A et al, EMNLP 2020.

Diverse Image Captioning with Context-Object Split Latent Spaces - Mahajan S et al, NeurIPS 2020.( recent one published in NeurIPS 2020)


Some other Implementations:
1) PyTorch: https://github.com/ruotianluo/ImageCaptioning.pytorch
2) TensorFlow: https://github.com/DeepRNN/image_captioning
3) Torch: https://github.com/karpathy/neuraltalk2
4) Torch: https://github.com/jcjohnson/densecap
