overview

  • 2.5d + 1d pool encoder
  • ensemble of 6 Unet models

model architecture

  • Grouping the images by threes.
    • z_len = img_num / 3
    • images (batch_size*z_len, 3, height, width) →2dcnn encoder →reshape to (batch_size, z_len, output_channel, height, width) →avg pooling(z_axis) → decoder
  • decoder: Unet
