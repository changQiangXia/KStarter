We at the NFL league office are excited to be back with our seventh annual data science competition. This year our theme is intended to breakdown all things pre-snap. In NFL terms, that's taking the action before the play to learn about what happens during the play.

For folks wanting a job in sports analytics, consider this your opportunity/resume builder. And if you are new to the field, welcome! We're excited to follow along over the next few months, and if you have any questions about the themes, data set, or grading process, feel free to ping us here!

We'll leave you with some pro-tips on how to build a submission: 




Keep things simple. The NFL has been around for decades, so don't try to solve the sport all at once. Pick something small -- a position, a play type, a scheme, etc -- and try and do it well. 


The more football context, the better. And we have generally seen success when folks with strong football backgrounds mix with folks with strong coding backgrounds. 


Andrew Patton from our league office data science group has a bunch of thoughts here that are worth checking out: https://x.com/anpatt7/status/1746209633562443948


Best of luck, and we can't wait to see your findings!
