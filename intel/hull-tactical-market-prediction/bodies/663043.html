Initial features

I wont stop here for a while, but I had a total of ~6800 features calculated. Since values itself were not very meaningful and their scale changes over time I focused on volatilities, trends, deviations, ratios and so on. Most of the features were calculated in relative values, so, the distribution doesnt shift over time.

In final submission I had ~130 selected, I think it could be less, but, for some reason my permutation experiments were not stable.

Target setup

I think I have seen this idea in @morodertobias notebook, to map allocations to returns and use it as a target. For example, instead of using actual returns you map negative returns to 0 and positive to 2.

Problem definition

After defining target this way you can solve the problem either as regression or classification. I ve decided to stick with regression, because, at the end of the day, classification here would be regression as well.

Target experiments

I made a total of 9 different targets, among them worth to notice:

  1. 17.507 LB optimization way, I took the top scoring notebook and run an optimization function on the whole train, but it ended up with a pity values, most of them were very close to 0
  2. Kaplan-Meier function from CIBMTR:
  3. After looking at hist of predictions I decided they are not enough extreme and lack values close to 0 or 2, so I decided to make top10% of positive returns 4 and bot10% of negative -2, but still clip predictions afterwards
  4. There were more experiments, by changing positive top% values to 20 or 15, but both of them suffered from volatility penalty, so I decided to dump it.

Also worth to notice that I was splitting 0 and 2, not by positive or negative return, but with positive or negative return compared to expected risk_free_rate of ~3.25%

Feature selection

After calculating feature longlist I have just fitted CatBoostRegressor on the whole dataset. From CatBoost I extracted PredictionValuesChange, LossFunctionChange, ShapValues, also Spearman correlation. After this I have calculated ranks of the features and by averaging ranks received final ranks which I used to pick features.

Worth to say that later I excluded Spearman from calculation, because it ranks were very uncorrelated with other estimations.

Here is an example of FI table head:

After this I ve just picked a subsets of 100/150/200/250 up to 400 features and ran an OOT estimation on my test folds. Except just a competition metric, I measured its components, distribution statistics, positive and > 1 rates

Training

Model selection:

Unfortunately, nothing fancy here, just a CatBoostRegressor(max_depth=5, iterations=2500). I have tried to run optimizer, but each experiment was about 10 minutes and I run only a hundred iterations and they didnt beat above setup after postprocessing.

Window selection (how often to retrain)

Since initially I have experimented with 90 window size, I have also tested an obvious idea of reducing the window size to something reasonable, like 10 or 20. Yeah, it gave good boost on backtest, but I could not use it for online training, since made everything the last day and all my subs failed because of me forgetting to comment debug cells.

Postprocessing

This is a fun part, since it gave a lot of boost to the competition metric. After looking at hist plot of raw prediction:

I thought, why not just put to 0 all prediction below threshold, same as we would do in classification. It gave big metric boost:

On this example raw strategy had a metric of 1.07 and postprocessed with a simple threshold 1.67.

After this I decided to make more fancy postprocessings, like, finding an optimal allocation value in predictions bin - I have split prediction range into 6 bins [0.0, 1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.8, 2.0], 0-1 range always predicted as 0 and others had an options to pick from 0.5 to 1.5 with a step of 0.05. Sometimes it was better then simple threshold, but sometimes not, varied on experiment.

I ve also made something similar, but with prediction multiplication.

I decided to track strategy performance on rising markets and preferred them, to prevent overfitting for loss reduction. For example this one measured on public test, green had a lower competition metric:
