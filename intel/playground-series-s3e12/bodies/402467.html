  1. Extra Trees
  2. Hist Gradient Boosting
  3. Gradient Boosting
  4. LBGM
  5. Random Forest
  6. XGBoost

For Extra Trees, Random Forest and XGBoost, the feature combination was same. Rest was different. For Hist Gradient Boosting and LGBM, only 3 features combination(though different for both) worked.

On top of that, I ran the same VotingClassifier for different combination of subsets again and submitted the top 2 CV scores for scoring regardless of what they scored on public leaderboard.
