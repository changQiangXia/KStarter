Congratulations to all the winners! My solution builds upon the excellent work shared by @huikang  (https://www.kaggle.com/code/huikang/starter-notebook-select-patch-verify) 

My solution follows a straightforward yet intentional logic. During the competition, I observed that many top-performing teams had bypassed a significant number of edge cases (or "bugs"). Since I joined the competition relatively late, I made a deliberate choice to focus on identifying a reliable method to filter out high-risk bugs that would be impractical to address directly. Given the varying difficulty levels of different bugs, there exists statistical arbitrage potential in leveraging their inherent performance patterns — a key insight that guided my approach.

I first experimented with keyword-based filtering, such as excluding patches that returned terms like "error". However, this approach yielded only marginal improvements. A breakthrough came when I analyzed model performance across different weight configurations: I noticed that one specific set of model weights, despite failing on a large number of cases, maintained a notably high correct-to-wrong ratio in the cases it did successfully process. This consistency in its "successful attempts" became the core of my strategy — I centered my final submission on predictions from this model.
Fortunately, this focused approach paid off: both of my submissions using this selection logic secured a gold medal.
