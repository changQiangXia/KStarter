Common layers use Swish activation functions: 
Parallel branches (all the layers of the branche use the same activation function) swish, selu, relu.

Final solution is blending my best model to the submission file from @mehrankazeminia notebook with weights 50-50. resulting in a score of 0.99829 on public and 0.99825 on private.
