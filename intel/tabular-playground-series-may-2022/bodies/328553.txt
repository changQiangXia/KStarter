I don't even know where to start, since this is my first participation in the competition and it was very pleasant (and unexpected) for me to take 5th place. 
Congratulations to the winners. @ambrosm's two-branch solution is very unusual, I learned a lot from it.
My solution is somewhat simpler, I used a Catboost Langevin model with a depth of 8 and a huge regularization coefficient (this helps the training not get stuck in a dead point). 
Then I combined CatBoost model results with the Keras solution…

I used a modified Mish activation function, I made the activation more sensitive (be careful with it, it often gives a gradient vanishing):

 ():
     tf.keras.layers.Lambda( x: x*tf.keras.backend.tanh(+tf.keras.backend.log(tf.keras.backend.exp(x*))))(x)


I will be glad to answer your questions. Good luck to everyone in the next competitions.
