Hi everyone, 

Our host at OpenAI (@owenvallis) and Kaggle team would like to thank you for your interest and participation in the competition! If you are planning on submitting your writeup to be considered for the Working Note Award, here is how to do it:



Navigate to "Your Work", on the left side menu.

Click on the button "Create" that sits right under the page title "Your Work" and click "New Project Writeup". (This is a black button with white letters, different from the white button with a blue + sign and grey letters under the Kaggle logo on the top left corner.)

Create your Writeup. Don't forget to make it public!

Share your writeup on this thread by September 8 at 11:59 PM UTC. If you already have a note you've published, please feel free to share your link in the Comments now.


Please refer to the Evaluation section in the Overview tab for judging criteria. Winner announcements are expected around mid October.

Good luck!

Elizabeth
