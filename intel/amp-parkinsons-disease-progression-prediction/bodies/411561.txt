This is basically bagging with booststrap and helps a lot stabilize the results. The thing is that using peptides features boosted my baseline score by around CV: +0.3 / LB: +0.3. 
 After the deadline I added the visit_month leak features into this model and it booosted by a lot my scores, but I wasn't able to beat the baseline anymore. What I concluded is that the leak features are so much stronger than the peptides features that makes the model focus only on the magic feats.
Hosts, in a real world application, without any magic feature, this sampling strategy may work to extract a slightly better signal from the data.
