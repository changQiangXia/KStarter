My wife is a structural engineer full time. Here are some things that she said I thought would add to the context of this problem:


  

  
A really important feature of concrete is the ratio of WaterComponent to CementComponent.
  
The lower the WaterComponent to CementComponent ratio, the faster it dries and the stronger the concrete is in the long run. 
  
Super Plasticizer is an expensive additive to concrete that is typically only reserved when higher than normal strength concrete is needed. 
  
Coarse Aggregate is larger rocks that are added to the concrete, typically increases strength.
  
Fine Aggregate is smaller rocks that are added to the concrete, fills in what would normally be air pockets but not as influential on overall strength. But the more Fine you have the more water you need.
  
FlyAsh reduces the mixing water requirement and allows the concrete to gain strength over time and lower the upfront cost.
  
Blast Furnace Slag, as the amount of this increases the late age compressive strength increases.
  
There have been some studies on FlyAsh/BlastFurnace ratio but nothing very conclusive in terms of strength.
  


Hope you find this helpful in your feature engineering stage!
