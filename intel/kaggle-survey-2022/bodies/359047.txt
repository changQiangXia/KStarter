Topic #359047: Useful additional data sources for your story!

  Posted: 2022-10-10 16:05:06.357000
  Votes: 35  Comments: 6

This year's edition of the competition for the best notebook describing the data from this year's Kaggle survey has started, so probably many people are looking for an idea and inspiration for the subject of their notebook. As you can use additional data sources, I I wrote down some interesting suggestions in my opinion that can enrich our analysis if they fit the topic well. I invite you to a short overview!
1. Kaggle surveys from past years: 2021, 2020, 2019, 2018, 2017
The results from previous years' surveys fit well with all narratives related to time. If we want to see how the aspects described in the survey have changed over time, it is worth using the old data. The format and type of question has been heavily standardized since 2018, only data from 2017 require a large amount of data cleansing and recoding.
2. Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2011-2022
Every year for 11 years, a similar type of survey appears on StackOverflow, the largest forum for people using programming languages and more. The use of this data can be used to mention Python coders information in a Kaggle survey or be a starting point for comparing data workers to other IT parts.
3. World Development Indicators or others datasets about countries
If we want to do analyzes where the country of residence of the respondents is an important aspect, it may be a good idea to use other information about these countries (e.g. demographic, economic or social) to add additional context to our analyzes. There are a lot of such databases on Kaggle, just enter the keyword "countries" in the datasets tab and read the various databases and find out whether the information contained in them can be combined in an interesting narrative related to the Kaggle survey.
4. Meta Kaggle
Data on Kaggle itself has been used frequently in previous editions, especially when the notebook's narrative is focused on the Kaggle portal itself and how it works. In this database we will find gigabytes of data about competitions, users, databases, notebooks and discussions.
5. Data Science Job Salaries
If the narrative covers the financial aspect that is part of the survey, we also have other sources talking about this topic in the industry. Many portals publish data sicence job offers around the world with an indicative salary, which may bring interesting conclusions in conjunction with data from this competition.

There is a lot of data that can be used - it all depends on the subject of the notebook and its needs. If you have any additional suggestions, feel free to share in the comment :) 

Good luck in the competition!
