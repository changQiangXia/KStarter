At the end, some statistics of the notebook itself and smaller key aspects that did not fit into any of the categories in previous posts.



The development itself (code, descriptions) took about 70-80 hours, while the total effort related to thought processes is too hard to estimate in time (I would have to measure it on a regular basis)

It was my 3rd edition in which I submitted a notebook for the competition: 2019 (no prize, notebook &gt; 100 votes), 2020 (notebook award), in 2021 I created two very simple notebooks just for fun and I am not submitting any of them to the competition

I took part in 2 other analytical competitions, also without success, but they certainly helped me gain skills and intuition that helped me win here (so if in your first competition, despite a lot of effort, you didn't win anything, don't be discouraged, it always gets easier with time!)

I publicly released my notebook as more than 2 weeks before the end of time, mainly to get a chance to get feedback in the form of comments and upvotes and possibly change something based on this feedack

For the duration of the competition (knowing more or less the timeline in advance) I limited other unnecessary activities, I took care of all major private matters beforehand take 2 days off from work, to make sure that I would have enough time for the competition, and on the day of the end of the competition, to relieve myself, I flew for a quick vacation

I read the competition evaluation rules very carefully and based on them I tried to improve my notebook


I hope you found the above information useful and/or inspiring. Let me know what you think in the commentary or if you have any suggestions on how you think it's worth working on such problems, share in the comment - I'd love to read it!
