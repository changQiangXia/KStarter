Topic #519249: A formal request from the host to disqualify leak-based solutions
  Author: greySnow
  Posted: 2024-07-10 10:13:57.358000
  Votes: 51  Comments: 144

It turns out that due to pbuf_ozone_2 leak, test set timestamp and location can be exactly recovered. This leak enables 2D-&gt;1D models and possibly also future data utilization (using the input for future samples to 'predict' the output of a previous one).
I humbly request the hosts disqualify all techniques that explicitly utilize this (and possibly other similar) leak. It was repeatedly stressed by the hosts that this competition is to be a 1D-&gt;1D regression problem, and I, and I think this is also true for many others, invested extensive time and effort (and possibly money for compute) based on this assumption. All of this will become meaningless now if teams start to utilize the leak.
On a personal note, I made a lot of interesting things and utilized advanced techniques in this competition, which I would keep secret for future competitions if I drop from the price range due to a leak, as I'm sure that those techniques would easily lend me gold in future relevant competitions.
