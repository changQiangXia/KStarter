Hello all,

Wishing you all the best for the challenge! This is a regression challenge aimed at imputing missing values in the provided dataset. Original data is located at- https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/saurabhshahane/predicting-heat-flux 
This competition is very similar to TPS June2022 in objective.
