Model Overview

My approach follows the methodology outlined in this tutorial and this previous Solution. I used linear regression to model the trend and trained an XGBoost model separately for the residuals.  

A clear periodic pattern was observed:  



Kaggle and Kaggle Tiers exhibited a two-year cycle

Other products followed a one-year cycle


Based on this, I created one pair of sine and cosine features per product to capture seasonality.  

To apply linear regression effectively, I transformed the target variable using the logarithm to express the relationship in an additive form.  

The model estimates total sales (total_num_sold), but I struggled with making accurate predictions for 2018 and 2019. While the Leaderboard (LB) score was reasonable, the forecasts for these years were inaccurate, leading to a drop in the Private LB Score.  

After extensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), I implemented an imputation strategy for missing values, leading to a significant improvement in both the Public and Private LB scores.  

Mathematical Representation

The model structure can be expressed as follows:  

$$
\textit{num_sold} = (\textit{rel_gdp}) \cdot (\textit{total_sum}) \cdot (\textit{store}) \cdot \left[\sin(\textit{product}) + \textit{weekend_impact} + \textit{holiday_impact} + b_0\right]
$$

Where:  

$$
\textit{weekend_impact} = \text{XGBoost}_{\textit{weekend}}(\textit{features})
$$

$$
\textit{holiday_impact} = \text{XGBoost}_{\textit{holiday}}(\textit{features})
$$
