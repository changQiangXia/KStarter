One point that may affect how you use the data: the two MABe22 datasets are collections of one-minute clips sampled at random from a group of experiments that recorded mouse behavior 24 hours a day for several days. The upshot of this is that mice sleep- so some of these clips are pretty uneventful! Furthermore, mice also love to sleep in a big pile called a huddle, which makes pose estimation during these times quite noisy.

If you would like to exclude these clips from your analysis, simply check the "mouse1 condition" field of the csv: if it says "lights on" then the mice (who are nocturnal) are more likely to be sleeping. Or you can look at the tracking data itself and just toss out clips where there's no movement.
