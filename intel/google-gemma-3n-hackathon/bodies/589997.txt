Hey all,

We mistakenly listed the license for public writeups in this competition as CC0 (public domain) instead of what it really was- CC BY 4.0 (Attribution). This was noted correctly in the product, but incorrectly in the rules. I've updated the rules accordingly.

If this changes your intent for your writeup to be licensed under CC BY 4.0 at the end of the competition, you are welcome to unsubmit your writeup and it won't be judged.

Thanks for understanding!
