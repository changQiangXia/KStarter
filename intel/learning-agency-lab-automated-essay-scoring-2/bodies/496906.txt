Has anyone tried using the more training data available in Persuade 2.0 corpus dataset here? This competition provides 17307 train samples. It has been shown that 12873 of these samples are exact duplicates (both essay and target) from the Persuade 2.0 corpus by Abdullah here and confirmed by me here.

More Train Samples in Persuade 2.0 Corpus

Since our train data overlaps the Persuade 2.0 corpus, has anyone tried training directly on the 25996 samples in Persuade corpus? It seems that the essays and targets are the same as provided as train data in this competition. This gives us an additional 8689 samples to train our models with. I suspect this will boost CV scores and LB scores!

Auxiliary Training

Furthermore, the Persuade corpus has additional columns: ['prompt_name', 'task', 'assignment', 'source_text', 'gender', 'grade_level', 'ell_status', 'race_ethnicity', 'economically_disadvantaged', 'student_disability_status']. We do not have these columns for test data but during training we can predict these columns as auxiliary targets. This can make our models smarter because we challenge our models to learn more things at once. Then during submission to LB, we ignore the prediction of these columns.

Feature Engineering

The best public notebook here creates many features and trains an LGBM model. Some features are the 6 target probabilities from training DeBERTa. Has anyone tried to predict the additional Persuade corpus columns of ['prompt_name', 'task', 'assignment', 'source_text', 'gender', 'grade_level', 'ell_status', 'race_ethnicity', 'economically_disadvantaged', 'student_disability_status'] and input the probability predictions for each of these columns into the LGBM as additional features?

Is our Test Data LB Targets in Persuade Corpus?

Since our train data overlaps with Persuade corpus, is it possible that some of the answers for the test data LB overlap with Persuade corpus (i.e. competition leak)? 

Answer: NO, i don't think so.

I have probed LB with the notebook here. During submission, this notebook compares our competition's test data to the Persuade corpus. If/When it finds any matches, it will use the target from the Persuade corpus as prediction. This notebook did not receive any benefit which supports the idea that our test data does not overlap the Persuade corpus.
