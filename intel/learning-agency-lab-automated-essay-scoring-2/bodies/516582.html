Thanks for the Kaggle and Competition Hosts for host this Interesting competition! My Secret for stable in LB/PB are:

  1. two stage training: First pretrain on Kaggle-Persuade data, then load the weights and train on Kaggle-Only data.
  2. using StratefiedKFold.
  3. validate on both Kaggle-Persuade(A) + Kaggle-Only(B). Search thresholds on A+B, and compute CV both on A + B and B.

I found my single models have good correlation some times(with CV on B, but not always). Like many others disscuss here, It's very hard to select final best sub, I also have best PB 0.844 didn't select.
