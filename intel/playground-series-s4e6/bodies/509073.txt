Topic #509073: The two most important features
  Author: AmbrosM
  Posted: 2024-06-01 04:08:37.708000
  Votes: 61  Comments: 20

Plotting a shallow decision tree is a good method for quickly understanding what are the most important features of a dataset:



The decision tree shows us that Curricular units 2nd sem (approved) and Tuition fees up to date are the two most important features:



If Curricular units 2nd sem (approved) is 5 or more, the student is classified as graduate.

If Curricular units 2nd sem (approved) is 0 or 1, the student is classified as dropout.

Otherwise, check the tuition fees. If they are up to date, predict enrolled; if they are not, predict dropout.


Source code is in the EDA which makes sense.
