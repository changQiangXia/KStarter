This post is dedicated to beginners and newcomers to ML and AI who could be looking for a roadmap to approach such assignments. This is a suggestion from my end and one could tailor this to one's needs and time availability, but this could be considered as a base/ blueprint for one's time and effort management in this competition.

I strongly recommend beginners and newcomers to start early, preferably towards the start of the competition. Starting late will reduce your experimentation time and your final solution may suffer thereby.

Approach roadmap

Divide your approach into 3-4 steps as below, focusing on effective time management throughout-

Starting stage - exploration and ideation

  • This should be done in the first 2-3 days of the competition
  • Peruse public materials, including shared kernels and discussions
  • Completely avoid the blind blend kernels and focus on meaningful stuff in the code forums. Focus on kernels that perform EDA and discuss ideas rather than focusing on the score only.
  • This will provide sufficient ideas of models that work and don't work on the data and the associated good/ moderate features as well. Focus on the discussion forums for the CV-LB relations and other useful stuff for you to ideate and think through

Single models

  • Experiment across feature sets, add and remove features based on the CV-score.
  • If you are struggling with code writing, use a coding agent like Google antigravity and Claude Code to assist you with the coding endeavor. Iterate through various feature combinations and iterate quickly
  • Construct meaningful single models and note down the CV scores across individual model options. Number your experiments well and save the out-of-fold predictions and the test set submission files properly
  • You could submit single models and check the CV-LB relation here - this is important to determine effective and ineffective model options
  • Don't delete low performing models until and unless you spot an error - these could be useful additions in an ensemble later

Blend

  • This is an iterative process again, indulge in cv-backed blending processes every 3-4 days when you have a reasonable amount of single models from your past iterations
  • Focus on various blending options - a lot of this is discussed in past playground solutions and highly popular posts across the forums
  • Build the ensemble using the same CV scheme as the single models for consistency
  • Submit these blends for your convenience and note down the CV-LB consistency
  • Try and experiment through the month so that your model pipeline improves consistently

Final submission choice

  • Select your final submissions based on the CV-LB score
  • We have 2 submission choices - be wise to hedge the risk by selecting diverse model choices - hence experimenting a lot helps
  • Document your process and publish it (despite the end result) - this will help you fathom your own process and understand where you need to improve the next month.

Wishing you the best for the assignment and a happy holiday period ahead!
