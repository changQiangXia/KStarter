Topic #604028: Are 50% of Kaggle Playground Regression Competitions created from Random Targets?
  Author: Chris Deotte
  Posted: 2025-09-05 20:01:01.891000
  Votes: 64  Comments: 29

Each Kaggle Playground competition is synthetic data generated from an original dataset. Is it the case that many Kaggle Playground competition original datasets are just random numbers with no signal?

We can evaluate original datasets with an XGB model. We can train an XGB on the original data features and target. Then we randomly shuffle the target and train 100 more XGB models. Lastly, we compare the CV RMSE scores of the XGB trained with original target versus the XGB trained with the random target.

If the CV score from using the original target is within z-score -2 to 2 of the CV score from using random targets, we conclude that the original data is just random numbers. In the past 6 regression competitions, 50% or 3 out of 6 were just random numbers :-(

Synthetic Data Generation Adds Signal

Note that the existence of original datasets with random numbers doesn't mean that the associated Kaggle playground competitions are a random lottery and modeling doesn't help. This is because the process of generating synthetic data adds signal (explained here and here). But Kaggle playground competitions created from random numbers are boring :-( The data has no real patterns and the data has no relationship to real life :-(
