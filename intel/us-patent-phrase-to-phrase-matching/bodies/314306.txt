Topic #314306: Wondering what the code in "context" means? I compiled everything in a CSV for you
  Author: xhlulu
  Posted: 2022-03-22 02:27:32.563000
  Votes: 173  Comments: 33

If you look at the context column in the training data, you'll find codes like A47 and F16. If you click on the link to the wikipedia page on cooperative patent classification, you'll find the following example:


  
The first letter is the "section symbol" consisting of a letter from "A" ("Human Necessities") to "H" ("Electricity") or "Y" for emerging cross-sectional technologies. This is followed by a two-digit number to give a "class symbol" ("A01" represents "Agriculture; forestry; animal husbandry; trapping; fishing").


The Wikipedia page has a nice list of sections, but how do we know what are the names of each class? The competition data links to codes meaning on the USPTO website, but it's only available in PDF/interactive format… But it turns out that you can download this data on the CPC website at the link CPC Title List (2022.02). 

Since the format is a bit hard to work with, I downloaded, cleaned it up, and generated this aggregated dataset containing the meaning of all CPC codes. Hope this helps!
