The focus of the 2022 Big Data Bowl is special teams and the NFL Football Operations group has put together demos to help participants get started. These demos will guide you through how to measure a place kicker's offset from center. 

To understand the importance of this metric first check out this article: Comparing Kickers Across the League on Accuracy Between the Uprights

Tutorial using R: Analyzing Place Kicker Offset from Center - R 
Tutorial using Python: Analyzing Place Kicker Offset from Center - Python

Using these tutorials you can learn how to read data, clean data, animate data, and plot data!



We encourage you to reach out with any questions you have. Good luck to everyone!
