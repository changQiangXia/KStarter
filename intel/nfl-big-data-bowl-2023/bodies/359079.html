Linemen in football are like 300 pound car mufflers -- by and large, you only hear about them when something's gone wrong.

That is, until the 2023 Big Data Bowl.

The NFL league office's data science competition is back with our fifth event. This year, we're sharing Next Gen Stats tracking data for dropback pass plays (Weeks 1-8) of the 2021 season, with the goal of evaluating linemen (both offensive and defensive) performance. Each play has player tracking (location, speed, acceleration, direction) for each player on the field, from the moment of snap until the pass has been released, as well as PFF scouting data to provide context into player responsibilities.

Your job? Tell us about linemen performance.

As is outlined on the evaluation page, this year's competition is an "analytics one" (no leaderboard), and submissions can fall under one of three tracks. New this year is a "Coaching" theme -- where we encourage participants to team up with a coach to generate insight and to better understand team strategy.

We'll have more to come in the next few weeks, but we look forward to your participation and learning from this community!
