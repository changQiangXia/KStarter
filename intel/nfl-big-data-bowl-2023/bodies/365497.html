Hello,

Hopefully everyone is starting to get used to the data! To help some who are figuring out their topic we put together a list of some potential topics. This list is just a small subset of possible topics and there are many other great potential topics that are not included here that we would encourage participants to do.

Ideas Offense:
• Classify Lineman Strategies (Man Duel, Slide Protections, Empty)
• Analyze offensive team ability to determine strong/weak side and slide to given direction.
• Analyze tendencies of running backs to run a route or block given defensive pass rush
• Analyze blocker stance and how it ties to whether pass rushers get inside or outside
• Analyze how blockers without initial defender to block position themselves
• Analyze blocker positioning after Quarterback leaves the pocket and/or is pressured
• Analyze blocker ability to hold a defender in place without moving towards QB
• Determine how blocking assignments shifted from pre snap to end of play
• Link false start rates to offensive line get off
• Offensive tendencies home (using snap count, potentially) vs away (silent count)
• Link between quarterback release time, receiver openness, and offensive line performance

Ideas Defense:
• Identify defensive stunts/twists
• Identify who is most likely to pass rush given pre-snap positioning
• Identify players who most attract additional blocking help (i.e. Aaron Donald, TJ Watt)
• Analyze what blitzing strategy can best create pressure while keeping number of pass rushers constant
• Link offsides/neutral zone infraction rates to pass rusher get off
• Defensive tendencies home (using snap count, potentially) vs away (silent count)
