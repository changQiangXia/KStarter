Hello Everyone
I have created a custom dataset by combining images from different sources. The dataset includes a total of 9 classes :

1. apparel
2. artwork
3. dishes
4. furniture
5. landmarks
6. others (memes, illustrations, cars combined)
7. packaged_goods
8. storefronts
9. toys


Link to the dataset - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/odins0n/guie-custom-data

Each category consists of at most 1000 images and all images are available in two dimensions - 128x128 and 224x224. The images are collected from different kaggle datasets and sources. Find the full list here- Click Here.

I have created a simple Baseline notebook as well. Check it out here - (To be updated soon) 
I will keep adding more images to the dataset…
