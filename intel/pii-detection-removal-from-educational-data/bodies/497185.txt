Stage 1: Select candidates 
Stage 2: Score them.

--

Base Ensemble:
v3-xsmall 256/512/1024/2048; v3-small 768; v3-base 1280; 
mdeberta-v3-base 960

nbroad &amp; mpware datasets, augmentations, START END and NEWLINE tags

~0.964-0.972 3-fold CV

--

Stage 1 RNN
deberta-v3-xsmall (embeddings, 384)
 -&gt; RNN(384 -&gt; 64, bidirectional) -&gt; LayerNorm
 -&gt; interleave fwd/bkwd -&gt; Conv(128, 128, k = 13, groups of 64) -&gt; Tanh 
        -&gt; (add to rnn output) -&gt; LayerNorm
-&gt; RNN(128 -&gt; 64, bidirectional) -&gt; LayerNorm -&gt; Linear(128, 13)

very fast ~120k/s = ~2 mins

~0.93 CV

.

Stage 2 Mini-Attn-Rnn
~3% of tokens, based on a 1e-3 cutoff and ~2-16 token proximity

deberta-v3-xsmall embeddings
  -&gt; Attn(dim = 320, copied from v3-xs) 
    -&gt; partial feed-forward, 
  -&gt; GRU(384, 96, bidirectional) -&gt; [as above]
        -&gt; Conv, [as above]
  --&gt; GRU(192, 96), [as above]

~15k/s - 25k/s, (&gt;500k/s effective speed) = &lt;1min

~0.94 CV

.

Stage 3: V3-XS
 ~3% tokens, as above
deberta-v3-xsmall, 8 layers 
~2k/s (&gt;60k/s effective speed) = 2-3mins

~0.965 CV

.

All models were trained with distillation of base ensemble, annealing from softened logits to half-labels, half-logits.

Out-of-fold 'filter probabilities' used to train the two filtered models.

Seqlens of 256 for the RNN, 320 for the mini-attn, and 640 for the final model.

Both stage 2 and stage 3 use SEP tags between token spans, and deberta-v2 was rebuilt with custom indexing to preserve the original positional indexes.
