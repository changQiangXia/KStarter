I'm Mocchan, and I took 4th place!

Like the 1st person, I also happened to find out that deep learning has a big impact on the public score this time.
I was surprised at first because the targets made by deep learning raised my score even though there was no correlation with XGBoost or EDA.

I set out to increase the public score for deep learning. This was the KMeans method for this purpose, and I apologize if I have confused you.

Here is the document that I used as a reference for deep learning.
https://www.kaggle.com/kavehshahhosseini/tps-oct-2021-multi-input-neural-network
