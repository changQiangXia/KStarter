I used the Hugging Face Inference API to make 8.5k samples of prompts with two responses from various open models.  The prompts were taken from the LMSYS 1M dataset. There is no winner column, but pseudolabeling was very useful in the last LMSYS competition, so it will likely be important here.

Dataset here

I haven't yet tried it out, so please do share what you find! I may update it later with more samples and newer models if people find it useful.
