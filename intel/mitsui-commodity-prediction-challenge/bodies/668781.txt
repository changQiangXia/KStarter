Strategy

Like many other participants, I expected this contest to be a kind of "lottery." Furthermore, such time series tasks require great care to avoid failure of submissions during the prediction phase. With these things in mind, I decided to include a very simple solution in one of my submission and not to spend too much time on this competition. As a result, this simple solution worked best for me.

Solution

I used the rank mean for the prediction. Below is my solution:

Data Loading &amp; Configuration Block

train_labels = pl.read_csv("/kaggle/input/mitsui-commodity-prediction-challenge/train_labels.csv")
NUM_TARGET_COLUMNS = 424
TARGETS = [f"target_{i}" for i in range(NUM_TARGET_COLUMNS)]


Predictor Definition Block

predictor = train_labels[TARGETS].to_pandas().rank(axis=1).mean()


Prediction Block

def predict(
    test: pl.DataFrame,
    label_lags_1_batch: pl.DataFrame,
    label_lags_2_batch: pl.DataFrame,
    label_lags_3_batch: pl.DataFrame,
    label_lags_4_batch: pl.DataFrame,
) -&gt; pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame:

preds = {}
for i in tqdm(range(NUM_TARGET_COLUMNS)):
    preds[f"target_{i}"] = predictor[i]

return pl.DataFrame(preds)
