Topic #599772: This competition is most likely completely based on luck
  Author: paperxd
  Posted: 2025-08-18 22:02:24.227000
  Votes: 33  Comments: 15

I simulated half a million random submissions to see how well a random predictor would perform on the dataset. Without data leakage, most models achieve around a 0.2-0.3 score on the Public Leaderboard. However, the simulated results below show a mean score of 1e-5, which is negligible enough to be considered 0, a standard deviation of 0.107, a minimum of -0.49 and a maximum of 0.55. This means any score within the interval (-0.302, 0.302) might be attributed to your luck. A random predictor even scored 0.55, so if you get lucky and get that 0.000002% jackpot, this might be all it takes to win the competition.
