Welcome to I’m Something of a Painter Myself!

We are super excited to have you participate in our first-ever generative getting started competition. Your task is to generate Monet-esque images, whether from scratch or by transforming photos. To learn more, check out our competition description.

As a Getting Started Competition, there are no prizes, deadlines, nor private leaderboards. This competition will run indefinitely and will be a resource to become more familiar with these concepts:

  • Computer vision
  • Generative models
  • Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)
  • TFRecords format for TensorFlow

To get started, we have created a CycleGAN tutorial on the data, but please feel free to experiment with other architectures as well. You are welcome to add in additional datasets to help with the training of your model; just remember that submissions of actual Monet paintings and transformations of such paintings are not allowed.

Good luck and have fun learning to paint (using computer vision, that is)!

Remember: Kaggle is for everyone. Whether you're teaming up or sharing tips in the competition forum, we expect everyone to follow our Kaggle community guidelines.
