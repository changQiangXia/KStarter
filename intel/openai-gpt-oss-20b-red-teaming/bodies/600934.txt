Hi all,

With the competition just two days from closing, we have a reminder and an update to share.

Reminder: Be sure your full Writeup with all attachments is fully submitted by the competition deadline. This means your project should appear as Submitted not as Draft. If you have any incomplete items (e.g. missing a header image) you will receive an error and your Writeup will not be received.

Update on Public Data Assets: At the request of the host and as raised by many participants due to the sensitive nature of the competition, we will be keeping all Kaggle assets private until the judges have a chance to review them and remediate if necessary. This means that if your Writeup, attached Kaggle Datasets and/or Kaggle Notebooks are not public after the deadline, this is working as intended and is not a cause for alarm. The Kaggle team will work with the Hackathon judges team to provide access for review, and we will ensure all Writeups and assets are made public once the judges team has reviewed.

Happy Modeling!
