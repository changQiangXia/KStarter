  Posted: 2025-08-05 16:51:00.970000

Mighty Kagglers,

First off, it’s very nice to be back in the Kaggle community – I’ve missed you!  

I do love my new role working on Safety Systems at OpenAI and am really excited to help bring this Red Teaming Challenge based on gpt-oss-20b to Kaggle.  I have always believed that there is a special power to the global community of Kaggle, and that there is nothing quite like bringing so many brilliant minds from across the world together to work on problems like safety testing.

The gpt-oss-20b model is, in my opinion, a truly exciting model.  It’s small enough to run locally but powerful enough to do some serious reasoning.  You can read all about it in the official blog post, review the safety paper and model card with all of the safety testing that was done before release, and check out the gpt-oss cookbook to see different ways to access the model and start playing with it – including directly through gpt-oss.com.

Having a new, open source model of this quality for a red teaming challenge is an amazing opportunity for us to move the whole science of safety testing forward as a field, together.  Special thanks to the good folks at OpenAI for supporting this challenge.

I am also deeply appreciative of the amazing judging panel that has volunteered to help in this effort– it’s an all-star group from across the industry, and we hope that this is the first of several in a series of similar challenges hosted by different organizations.

But most of all I’m looking forward to seeing what you discover, what methods you invent, and what we can learn together from this challenge.  Thank you, and good luck!

Very best wishes,
