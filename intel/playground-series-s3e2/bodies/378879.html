I trained 5 CatBoost, XGB, LGBM, and Lasso classifiers with StratifiedKFold (k = 5) and submitted two blends. First with equal weights, second with Lasso having double weight compared to the rest. Even weights performed better.

Code: https://www.kaggle.com/code/vjpkaggle/stroke-prediction-playground-s3e2

After finding suitable parameters for the boosters, I added a small grid search for each fold to allow slight variation in the parameters across the models. This ended up increasing private scores by only ~0.0002 in both cases. This mix was also slightly better than my submissions with any of the individual models, although LGBM alone got within 0.0002.

From all the blends that I tested and submitted, the best performing one gave CatBoost twice as much weight as any of the others.

For preprocessing, I tried adding the discussed rick factor count, but in quick testing it did not improve CV scores so I did not include it in the final model.

I also ordinal encoded smoking_status instead of one-hot encoding it after testing both and finding a slight improvement in CV scores with ordinal encoding.
