Solution: After reading the comment by @siukeitin that, in the original dataset, the target is 0.1*(mean of features) I realized that the whole dataset was unneeded, and it was a question of modelling the artifacts due to the synthetic data generation.
To this end I created the mean feature to model the signal, and the median, min, max and std features to model the synthetic 'noise', then dropped all of the original data.

These five features were then fitted using CatBoost (hyperparameters: n_estimators=2000, although the default 1000 would have done)
