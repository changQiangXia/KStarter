If we plot the sum of all features against the target, we see that sums 72, 73, 74 and 75 are special and lead to a higher flood probability:

Decision trees cannot guess that they need to consider the sum of twenty features. We have to help them by adding a suitable feature manually:


   [train, ]:
    [] = [initial_features].(axis=1) 
    [] = [].isin(np.arange(72, 76))

The increase in the validation score of untuned CatBoost is huge:

  • Without the new feature: 0.84722
  • With the new feature: 0.86840
