Further to my references post, I may add the below risk factors for pertinent blood test elements involving cholesterol and triglycerides based on the data-

Bilirubin

  1. Normal level < 1.2 milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL) - adults and <1 milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL) for less than 18 years
  2. Jaundice >= 2 1.2 milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL)

Total Serum Cholesterol

  1. Normal level - <200 mg/dl
  2. Borderline level - 200-239 mg/dl
  3. High level - >=240 mg/dl

Albumin

  1. Normal level - 3.4 to 5.4 g/dL
  2. Liver/ kidney disease - < 3.4 g/dl
  3. Other surgical complications- > 5.4 g/dl

Copper levels

  1. Normal level - 62 to 140 micrograms per deciliter (mcg/dL)
  2. Liver/ kidney disease risk - > 140 micrograms per deciliter (mcg/dL)

Alkaline Phosphatase- Alk_Phos

  1. Normal level - 44 and 147 U/L (adults)
  2. Bone/ Liver diseases - > 147 U/L

SGOT

  1. Normal level - 8 and 45 units per liter of serum
  2. Disease level - > 50 for cisgender males and > 45 for female

Tryglicerides

  1. Normal level - < 150mg/dl
  2. Borderline high- 150 -199 mg/dL
  3. High - 200 - 499 mg/dl
  4. Very high - > 499 mg/dl

Platelets

  1. Normal level - 150,000 to 450,000 platelets per microliter of blood
  2. Bleeding issues - < 150,000 platelets per microliter of blood

Prothrombin

  1. Normal level - 11- 13.5
