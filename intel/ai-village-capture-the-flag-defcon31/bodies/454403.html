Passphrase

The key for solving passphrase was realizing that there was a sentiment model in Huggingface with almost the same predictions as the API.

Then my first idea was to try to understand the differences between that model and the API. But I found to my surprise that the differences were tiny and there was no pattern.

Then I realized that the model allowed to have more precision on the predictions. Thus a simple greedy search of a passphrase with the same score was enough to solve the challenge: exciting four

Hush

On a first step I tried all sort of audios: frequencies, different kinds of noise and text to speech using the library espeak.

Words seem the kind of audio that got more varied and higher activations for hush. So I started sending random sentences and also riddles because they were mentioned in the task description. I collected a huge number of riddles from many sources.

The riddle that got the higher activation (summing all the activations returned by the API) was The more you take, the more you leave behind. Then I decided to create a gradio demo to record and send audio to the API. I notice that:

  • The order of the words is relevant, if I change the order of the sentences the activation changes a lot.
  • The order of the sentences is also relevant.
  • Delays at the beginning or end of the audio do not seem relevant
  • The voice does not matter, if different voices say the same the activation is similar
  • Small variations over the riddle had similar scores

So it seemed that the API was using speech to text, probably whisper and we had to guess the message.
I also tried running a whisper code attack, so I believe the model is whisper tiny

I started searching for sentences similar to the riddle and after a long time googling and chatting with ChatGPT I saw a sentence that I instantly knew it was the solution: the quieter you become, the more you are able to hear (the lema of Kali Linux)

Inversion

I tried many techniques to try to understand what each dimension of the output was looking for. But
I could not find anything that activated dimensions 4,5,7.

After many thoughts, hypothesis, brute force searching… I realized that those dimensions were hidden, they could be any letter and they could be different letters. With that in mind and the hint the AI is trying to tell us something! I suddendly saw the solution: letmeout

Pixelated

This was also a very nice challenge. First experiments showed that there was some OCR going on. Following the title of the task I decided to send pixelated versions of the original image and then I started seeing fails on the OCR and xlm code.

Talking with my assistant ChatGPT I was able to craft a payload to solve the challenge: hi </text> <is_admin>true</is_admin><text>. Then with opencv I created an image with that text and the challenge was solved.
