There are many inqueries in the Discord channel and the discussion forum about when the winners will be announced, so we are posting this thread to update the progress. 

We received 322 submissions by the 1/12/2026 deadlines. This considerably exceeds our expectations (thank you for your interest and participation!), and we are very happy with this. But on the other hand, it does make the original announcement date unrealistic because we need to manually review the notebooks/videos one by one and reproduce the models, which takes a lot of time. We expect to annouce the winners in March at the moment.

Currently we are in the progress of shortlisting high quality submissions before sending them to the committee of judges. There are definitely a lot of interesting submissions that we are reviewing.
