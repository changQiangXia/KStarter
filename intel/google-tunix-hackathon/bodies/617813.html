Hello everyone,

We (Tunix team at Google Cloud) are super excited that you are here to check out and participate in this new Tunix hackathon. Tunix is a new JAX-native LLM post-training library open sourced by Google in 9/2025 (launch blog). Tunix supports a wide range of post-training techniques on TPUs, and we are heads-down working on cutting-edge reinforcement learning for LLMs (reasoning training, agentic RL and etc.). In collaboration with the Gemma team, we are hosting this particular hackathon featuring finetuning Gemma models for general reasoning capabilities. And we are here to support you in this competition. Here are some resources to help you get started:

  • Tunix repository
  • Tunix documentation
  • Tunix examples (SFT, DPO, GRPO and etc.)
  • Gemma2 2B math reasoning training notebook on Kaggle
  • Gemma3 1B math reasoning training notebook on Kaggle

We cannot wait to see the creative solutions, recipes and artifacts you come up with in this hackathon. If you have any questions, feel free to start a thread in this discussion forum and we will try out best to address them.

-- On behalf of the Tunix team
