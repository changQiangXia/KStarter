I tried to visualize the public-private LB shakeup and found the below insights-

I split the scattergram of public-private LB results into several regions, each region describing associated specifics. Please find below a brief description of the same-

Region Description
1 These models did not work on any LB
2 They performed well on the public LB but got churned off in the private LB.
This illustrates the impact of the churn on top public LB submissions
3 Kudos to them! They performed well on both LBs. I wish to know if they performed well across the same submission/ different submission choices
4 Kudos to them as well! They performed well on the private LB from a moderate and tepid public performance. Perhaps their CV score must have been trustworthy.
5 Kudos to them as well! They performed well on the private LB from a low public score. Perhaps their CV score must have highly trustworthy.
6 This is the real impact of the churn. They performed well on the public LB but could not sustain on the private LB.
7 This is an interesting region and is a bit unique over recent episodes.
Quite a few participants retained consistent but moderate performance on both leaderboards.
Compared to regions 2 and 6, I think these participants performed well and this too is a success

Common inferences from regions 1-7:-

  1. Rely on CV and nothing else
  2. Try to be consistent but be wary of overfitting in such challenges. Synthetic data is noisy and very prone to overfitting
  3. Do not follow public submissions blindly. Extract value from across public work and incorporate them wisely in your work
  4. Try and submit diverse models as final submissions to hedge the risk of churning off.
