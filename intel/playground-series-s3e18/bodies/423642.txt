First of all, thanks to Kaggle for hosting the playground series and @mehrankazeminia and @tetsutani for sharing their notebooks, @onurkoc83 and @takshpanchal for teaming up!

In this post, I will be outlining my approach for S3E18. I treated the problem as two separate classifications instead of a multi-output classifier

In the preprocessing phase, I dropped HeavyAtomMolWt and fr_COO2 (since 99% of its values are same as fr_COO) and also removed erroneous records where FpDensityMorgan1 is -666. I then dropped the duplicate rows. The train set had 15824 records after preprocessing.

(1) https://www.kaggle.com/code/mehrankazeminia/ps3e18-gaussiannb
(2) https://www.kaggle.com/code/tetsutani/ps3e18-eda-ensemble-ml-pipeline-binarypredictict

EC1
I used an ensemble of CatBoost, LGBM and XGBoost, all gone through Optuna hyperparameter tuning. This ensemble gave a local OOF CV score of 0.71205

Subsequently, I blended this ensemble with (1) and (2) for the EC1 prediction 

EC2
Note that the ROC_AUC score for EC2 is only ~0.58, just slightly better than a classifier that predicts all 1s. I tried doing an ensemble but did not get a local CV score of &gt;0.592. I had the feeling that ensembles do not work well for EC2 and will overfit due to the low ROC_AUC scores of the base classifiers

As such, I just used the EC2 predictions from (2) for the prediction due to its relatively high CV score

This approach received a score of 0.66095, good for 11th place on the private leaderboard

My submission code is available here: https://www.kaggle.com/code/yeoyunsianggeremie/s3e18-11th-place-solution

Thanks for reading!
