Domain information- enzyme classification

  1. https://www.creative-enzymes.com/resource/enzyme-definition-and-classification_18.html -- outlines enzymes into 7 components and outlines associated reactions as well
  2. https://infinitabiotech.com/blog/classification-and-evolution-of-enzymes/ -- starter article regarding enzymes and their evolution and functions
  3. https://www.onlinebiologynotes.com/enzyme-classification-with-examples/ -- another good starter article for the same
  4. https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/enzymes-definition-structure-classification-examples/ -- outlines enzyme classification with very simple examples
  5. https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/319704 -- well explained article regarding enzyme classification with illustrations
  6. https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/getauthorversionpdf/C5OB00709G -- good paper to explain molecular complexity in detail
