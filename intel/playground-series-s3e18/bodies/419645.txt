Thanks to @gopalns for the original data

Note- only EC1 and EC2 need to be predicted for submission- 

Domain information- enzyme classification



https://www.creative-enzymes.com/resource/enzyme-definition-and-classification_18.html -- outlines enzymes into 7 components and outlines associated reactions as well

https://infinitabiotech.com/blog/classification-and-evolution-of-enzymes/ -- starter article regarding enzymes and their evolution and functions

https://www.onlinebiologynotes.com/enzyme-classification-with-examples/ -- another good starter article for the same 

https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/enzymes-definition-structure-classification-examples/ -- outlines enzyme classification with very simple examples 

https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/319704 -- well explained article regarding enzyme classification with illustrations

https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/getauthorversionpdf/C5OB00709G -- good paper to explain molecular complexity in detail


Multi-label classification



https://machinelearningmastery.com/multi-label-classification-with-deep-learning/ -- educates one on multi-label classification

https://vitalflux.com/difference-binary-multi-class-multi-label-classification/#:~:text=Multiclass%20Classification%20is%20where%20each,labels%20to%20each%20data%20sample. -- outlines the difference between binary, multiclass and multilabel classification

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-label_classification -- wiki article on multi-label classification

https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/an-introduction-to-multilabel-classification/ -- well drafted starter article regarding multi-label classification

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qMhNYVJH5Ag -- this video explains multi-label classification to great effect


Kaggle resources for multi-label classification from across competitions



https://www.kaggle.com/code/fchmiel/xgboost-baseline-multilabel-classification
