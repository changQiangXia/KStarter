Hi everyone. I have been planning on trying something (and sharing) but i have been busy (with other comps and work). So I will share the idea now and maybe someone will try before i have time.

Boosting Over Residuals

The idea of "boosting over residuals" worked very well in last Kaggle playground competition (using stage 1 model or formula trained on only original data, i.e. not comp synthetic data). So my idea is to try it again. (I realize that last comp was regression and this comp is classification, but it still works, read on).

Here is how to do it. First we train a model on the original data. Maybe an XGB or NN (or ensemble of both). Then we use that model (or ensemble) to make predictions on this competitions data. Call those pred0. 

Now we boost over residuals by creating a new target with new_target = pred0 minus original target. Then we apply my same idea from previous competition here.

Classification vs. Regression

I realize that last competition was regression and this competition is classification. But we can still boost over residuals because we convert our prior probability predictions into logits. Then we begin with these logits and ask our stage 2 (new) model to modify these logits. Lastly our stage 2 model converts these logits into new probabilities. (This works, I've done it before).

Good Luck! Have Fun!

If I have time, I will apply this and post a starter notebook. But for now, I wanted to share the idea so that people can begin exploring this idea. I'm confident that it will work and will become a common technique in every new Kaggle playground competition. (i.e. we build a model on original data, then boost it with a stage 2 "boost on residual" model on competition's synthetic data)
