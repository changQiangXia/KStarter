Image augmentation is an important method to increase the number of examples on your dataset. Especially where there is not enough data or it's hard to collect new samples, augmentation techniques can help a lot.

So, I went through some of the methods that have been used in the previous ISIC Competition by winning teams and wanted to display how they effect the input images. You can check the full code here.
