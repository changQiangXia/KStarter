First and foremost, we would like to thank the organizers (NVIDIA and kaggle) for hosting this incredibly challenging and unique competition. Congratulations to all the winners! 

While we competed across all tasks, our primary algorithmic and ML innovations focused heavily on the Bit Manipulation Puzzles, achieving &gt;96% validation accuracy (we expect it to reach &gt;98% with more training steps) —representing the highest performance in this category by any participating team.

To solve this, we abandoned arithmetic logic entirely. Here are the main innovations of our approach:



Bases and Truth Table Formulation: We reframed logic-gate deduction into a discrete feature-selection task. By leveraging string similarity (minimal bit flips) between outputs, we isolated primitive transformations ("bases") and deduced their truth tables without any complex arithmetic.

Backtracking and Error Recovery: We formalized a structured search process that tests candidate bases, detects logical collisions across a 64-row puzzle dataset, and deterministically backtracks upon failure to perform robust error recovery.

Bit Tokenization and Interactive Reasoning SFT: We bypassed standard tokenization to force the model to encode binary strings as individual, single-bit tokens (avoiding arbitrary chunking like 10100011 to [1010], [00], and [11]). Then, using Dynamic Loss Masking—training the model to natively hypothesize, self-evaluate, and backtrack.
