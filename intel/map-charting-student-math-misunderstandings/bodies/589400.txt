The train.csv data has 15 questions. And the 3 row sample test.csv has 2 questions which are included in the train.csv. Has anyone probed the LB to determine if the full LB test.csv contains any new questions, or does the full LB test.csv contain the same 15 questions?

This is important because if the full LB test.csv doesn't contain any new questions, then we can hardcode the correct answers for each question. Then we will always know if the label should begin with True or False for every one of our predictions.

Most Powerful Feature!

There are 12 Wrong Category Labels

In train data there are 12 wrong category labels. Namely 12 rows with QuestionId 31778 have MC_Answer 9 and Category True when the correct answer is 6. That is why we use tmp['c'] above to remove the incorrect answers.
