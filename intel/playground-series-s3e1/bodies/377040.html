Good luck to all! One suggestion is don't rely too heavily on the public leaderboard, especially because it only contains 20% of the test data. For those new to kaggle, a typical kaggle saying is, "trust your cv". There are often discussions about what that looks like in practice. One useful discussion from this competition is found here and another here.

For any of the winning and high-placed solutions to be revealed in a few hours, please share what worked for you and your process to get to the result you did. This can be very valuable to add to the learning process, especially for anyone who has been involved in this competition and even for kagglers who didn't enter this competition, it can still be a good learning opportunity.

Whatever result you get, try to take the positives from it in terms of lessons learnt and the final ranking especially if the final ranking isn't what you hoped. Once again, good luck.
