How to use latitude longitude for feature engineering

So on a different competition I found this interesting post about coordinates feature engineering:

Calculate distances to the important points to the map

  • If there is additional data about infrastructure/buildings, distance to the nearby shop, hospital, distance to the best school etc. If such data is not there, extract interesting points on the map from training/ test data. For example, divide the map into squares/grid, and within each square, find the most expensive flat, and for every other object in the square, add the distance to that flat. Or organize data points into clusters, and then use centers of clusters as important points. Or find some special areas, like the area with very old buildings and add distance to this one.

Aggregate statistics about surrounding area

  • Another approach is to calculate aggregated statistics for objects surrounding area. For example, number of flats around this particular point, representing area's popularity. Or add mean real estate price indicating how expensive area around selected point is.

Rotation of coordinates

  • If coordinates are used to train decision trees, slightly rotated coordinates can act as new features. It helps a model to make more precise selections on the map. Exact rotation needed needs to be found out, so we may want to add rotations like 45 or 22.5 degrees (etc). For example, say the street is dividing an area into two parts : high priced district above the street, and the low priced district below it. If the street is slightly rotated (line a inclined plane), trees will make lot of splits around the street. But if rotated coordinates are used, these two districts can be divided by a single split.
