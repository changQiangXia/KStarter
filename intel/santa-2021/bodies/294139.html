Keld Helsgaun and I used the traveling salesman problem (TSP) to verify the @cpmpml 2440 lower bound for non-wildcard solutions. We created an asymmetric TSP with 5281 nodes: one for each of the 5040 permutations, an extra 240 for copies of the 1-2 permutations, and a depot node. The travel costs are the permutation distances, except edges between copies of the same 1-2 permutations have cost 7, and the depot has cost 7 to all nodes and cost 0 from all nodes. Any Santa solution gives a TSP solution by concatenating the three tours, and the TSP solution has cost less than or equal to the sum of the lengths of the tours.

We used Concorde to build a linear-programming (LP) relaxation for the TSP. The relaxation has value 7320, which Concorde verifies with exact arithmetic. So any non-wildcard Santa solution has score at least 7320/3 = 2440. Remarkable that teams have found non-wildcard solutions of this length. Go combinatorics!
