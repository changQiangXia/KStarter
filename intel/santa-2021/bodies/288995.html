Traveling Salesman Starter - [LB 2500]

I published a traveling salesman starter notebook here. It installs and uses LKH-3 (Lin-Kernighan heuristic) from their webpage here

Basic Idea

The basic idea is that the long string we submit to Kaggle is an ordering of lots of "7 character permutations" small substrings. In the bottom of the image below, we see three permutations following each other, "1234567" (in red) then "4567312" (in orange) then "6731254" (in green).

We can think of each permutation as a "city". And we want each subsequent "city" to be close to the "city" in front of it because we want the overhang to be smallest length possible. Every pair of "cities" has a distance to go from A to B and a different distance to go from B to A. Our job is to visit all the cities in the shortest length possible.
