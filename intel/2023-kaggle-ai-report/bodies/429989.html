Congratulations everyone!

After evaluating all of your submissions, we are excited to announce the winners of the 2023 Kaggle AI Report Competition! We are truly grateful to everyone that shared an essay summarizing what the ML community has learned over the past 2 years.

We will provide awards to the top scoring essays in each of the following categories:

  • Text data
  • Image and/or video data
  • Tabular and/or time series data
  • Kaggle Competitions
  • Generative AI
  • AI ethics
  • Other

Text data:

In @abireltaief''s essay "Contemporary Large Language Models LLMs", the author provides a fantastic overview of large language models (LLMs), covering LLM prompting, LLM fine-tuning, and LLM application development. A small but well-curated collection of recently published papers are reviewed, and the concepts within are explained in an accessible and approachable manner. Thanks for contributing such an informative essay!

Honorable mentions:

  • http://www.kaggle.com/code/mistylight/mini-giants-small-language-models
  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/flaussy/large-language-models-reasoning-ability

Image and/or video data:

In @radbear's essay "Advances in AI Vision Models in the Last Two Years", the author discusses recent advances in semantic segmentation, and the development of vision transformers and continual learning methods, amongst other topics. The essay is well-referenced and provides a wealth of resources for those that want to dig into these topics in more detail. Thanks @radbear!

Honorable mentions:

  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/dokster/image-and-video-data-kaggle-ai-report
  • https://www.kaggle.com/omarrajaa/image-data-report

Tabular and/or time series data

In @rhysie's essay "Learnings From the Typical Tabular Pipeline", the author does a fantastic job of exploring the connections between the methods used in Kaggle competitions and the methods discussed in the academic literature. We really appreciated the deep-dive into Kaggle's monthly tabular playground series!

Honorable mentions:

  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/kensverse/ai-report
  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/kobbiemanrique/2023-kaggle-ai-report-tabular-data

Kaggle Competitions

In @iamleonie's essay "Towards Green AI", the author gives an overview of some AI research areas that are relevant to carbon footprints and the environment, and then explores the subset of Kaggle Competitions that specifically encourage submitting small and efficient models. Methods that can be used to reduce the carbon footprint of ML models are reviewed, and the essay leaves us feeling inspired to build more efficient ML systems. Thanks @iamleonie!

Honorable mentions:

  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/thedrcat/how-to-win-a-kaggle-competition
  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/nghihuynh/kaggle-ai-report-medical-imaging-competitions

Generative AI

In @trushk's essay "2023 Kaggle AI Report - Generative AI", the author surveys the landscape of generative AI products that have been released over the past few years. These exciting tools allow ML practitioners and even novices to generate images, text, videos, or code, and this essay provides a great overview of the major advancements that occurred along the way.

Honorable mentions:

  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/jay2333/understand-generate-and-transform-the-world
  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/pranavbelhekar/a-glimpse-into-the-realm-of-generative-ai/notebook

AI ethics

In Team TISL's essay "Exploring the landscape of AI Ethics", the author introduces and explains a diverse set of concepts related to ethics and AI. Terms such as bias, fairness, equity, and explainability are introduced and explained, and pressing issues are discussed, such as climate change, copyright issues, and government regulation.

Honorable mentions:

  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/antongc/developments-in-ai-and-ethics-in-the-past-2-years
  • https://www.kaggle.com/code/shreyamishra610/ethical-ai-is-all-we-need

Other

In @pluvias's essay, "Kaggle AI Report Optimization" the author explores recent advances in the fundamental area of optimization algorithms. The essay begins by giving an overview of more classical techniques, prior to moving on to highlight more recent advances such as MoMo and Sophia. Great work putting together this excellent resource @pluvia!
