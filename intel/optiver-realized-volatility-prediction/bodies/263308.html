Hey all,

It has been a little over a month since the competition has started. Here are a few observations and links to conversations that I have found useful so far in this competition.

  1. There have been talks about possible leaks in the data and in this competition. Whether that is true or not has yet to be 100% verified. However, in order to get below 0.20XX there is no real "magic" 🎩 or leaks. My best model is a single lightgbm model.

    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/256356
    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/256725

  2. As with most competitions one of the most important things is getting a good cross-validation scheme set up. In this competition I started with Kfold and then switched to GroupKfold split by time_id. I have had more consistency between my local validation and the LB with GroupKfold although not all decreases in CV translate to the LB. (Probably faulty features)

    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/260694
    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/256226
    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/256231

  3. Feature engineering and feature selection can go a long way. So far my biggest gains have been through feature engineering and feature selection. EDA has helped me quite a bit for figuring out and building different features. Feature selection is also important because of the constraints of the competition.

    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/256080
    • https://www.kaggle.com/c/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/discussion/255833

  4. I believe NNs can perform well here. I have no evidence to support this but if we just look at the history of recent competitions a NN with a creative architecture and data structure have peformed really well. I hope to build a few NNs at some point.

  5. If you have a lot of features & are encountering memory errors don't forget that you can change your dtype to np.float32 or np.int32 for example, that can help you manage your memory and build more feature if you have not yet done any feature selection or reduced your feature space.

I hope some of these observations are useful 🙂🧙‍♂️
