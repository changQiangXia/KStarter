What didn't work


Using the constructor that does not output uppercase made the score slightly worse.

Adding tokens like ["q" * n for i in range(16)] made the score worse.

Adding pseudo-labeled Feedback 2 or Feedback 3 data to the training data did not improve scores.

Changing the base model to the large model did little to improve scores.

Best CV: 0.61083
In the end, I created 8 models with 10 epochs training with all essays.

Ridge

I was lucky to find my key to this competition early. It was a Ridge model.
The ensemble, consisting of LGBM with 165 public and 51 of my features, a simple NN with two layers trained on the same features, and a Ridge model scored 0.561 on Private, which is strong gold. So I begin the description with Ridge. It was applied to Tfidf features with the following settings.

Feature engineering

I will only describe the features that I created in the hope that they will differ from the publicly available ones.

GridFeatureMaker

While most of the public notebooks calculate statistics for gaps, this class makes the same for fixed 60 time intervals 30 seconds each. PCA factors are calculated for selected statistics. I used PCA in order to 



Limit the number of features;

Make features different from those calculated for gaps.


KNNFeatureMaker

Only 3 but rather strong features. KNN regressor predictions based on 



statistics of the lengths of words;

statistics of text_change;

TfidfVectorizer features generated only by punctuation.

Nonlinear models

LGBM

I started with the brilliant notebook of @awqatak  Silver-bullet. Special thanks to him. After adding my features and additional tuning I got 10-fold CV 0.58880.

Simple NN with 2 FC layers and skip connection

It was trained on the same features as LGBM, 10-fold CV 0.59084

Deberta-v3-xsmall

After teaming up with @takuji, I modified his training code and achieved with deberta-v3-xsmall trained on lowercase reconstructed essays (the output of fast essay constructor) performance close to base and large models, 5-fold CV 0.611-0.613. This model was used for fitting to external datasets and obtaining external data features.

What didn’t work

Training LM with small vocabulary from scratch

Deberta tokenizer vocabulary doesn't contain tokens 'qqq','qqqq',… Here is the example

tokenizer.tokenize()



That’s why tokenizing essays we get very long sequences. We used max_len 2048 in our models. I created vocabulary consisting from punctuation, tokens like ‘q’*n and special tokens (55 tokens total), made a custom tokenizer for it, and tried to train deberta-v3-xsmall (and its variants with reduced dimension) from scratch on a large corpus with letters replaced by ‘q’ as MLM. I spent a lot of time on this project but failed. I couldn’t push the score below 0.63.

Text CNN

Best 5-fold CV was about 0.63 and it didn’t improve ensemble

Other NN architectures

I tried RNN, different variants of Transformer including the one presented by @pdnartreb in his winning solution with no success.
