Feature engineering

I will only describe the features that I created in the hope that they will differ from the publicly available ones.

GridFeatureMaker

While most of the public notebooks calculate statistics for gaps, this class makes the same for fixed 60 time intervals 30 seconds each. PCA factors are calculated for selected statistics. I used PCA in order to

  • Limit the number of features;
  • Make features different from those calculated for gaps.

KNNFeatureMaker

Only 3 but rather strong features. KNN regressor predictions based on

  • statistics of the lengths of words;
  • statistics of text_change;
  • TfidfVectorizer features generated only by punctuation.

External data features

What didn’t work

Training LM with small vocabulary from scratch

Deberta tokenizer vocabulary doesn't contain tokens 'qqq','qqqq',… Here is the example

tokenizer.tokenize()

That’s why tokenizing essays we get very long sequences. We used max_len 2048 in our models. I created vocabulary consisting from punctuation, tokens like ‘q’*n and special tokens (55 tokens total), made a custom tokenizer for it, and tried to train deberta-v3-xsmall (and its variants with reduced dimension) from scratch on a large corpus with letters replaced by ‘q’ as MLM. I spent a lot of time on this project but failed. I couldn’t push the score below 0.63.

Text CNN

Best 5-fold CV was about 0.63 and it didn’t improve ensemble

Other NN architectures

I tried RNN, different variants of Transformer including the one presented by @pdnartreb in his winning solution with no success.
