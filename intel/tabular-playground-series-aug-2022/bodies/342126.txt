I was trying to aggregate some of the features in an attempt to get some useful features. 
From the data the features could be grouped into material properties, dimensions, measurement conditions, loading, etc. Therefore, there is a good chance of creating a new one by combining some of them. Here are the things I tried.



Multiply length dimensions to get area. attributes 2 and 3 look like they are width and length dimensions or similar.

Aggregate measurement_3 to 16 into average and stdev. They look like they belong to the same group. 


Using Logistic regression and 10 features (7 original, 3 derived) I was able to get an LB of 0.58926. I am sure there is more if we can get them.
