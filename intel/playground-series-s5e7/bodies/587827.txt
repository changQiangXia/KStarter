  Author: Tilii

I have been analyzing samples that are difficult to predict. There is some relatively good news and some really bad news. The relatively good news is that there are just over 500 of those difficult samples. The really bad news is that there isn't much we can do to fix them, because they look exactly like samples from the opposite class.

I will first borrow a plot from my earlier post showing density distributions of features against the target values. This will hopefully help us see why the samples listed below are exactly the opposite from what they should be.
