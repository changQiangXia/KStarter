We’re excited to share an inference workflow for RNAPro for part 2 of the Stanford RNA 3D Folding competition ! RNAPro reflects where the field landed after last year’s breakthroughs. 

What is RNAPro?

RNAPro is a state-of-the-art RNA 3D folding model developed through a synthesis of ideas from the previous Kaggle competition hosts and top-performing teams. It integrates several components that proved valuable in the previous competition and subsequent work: 



RibonanzaNet2 as an encoder for sequence and pairwise features

RNA MSAs to provide rich input data beyond a single sequence

3D structure templates 

RNA fine-tuned Protenix to take the above inputs and predict 3D structure


This combination represents some of the best insights from the previous competition !

⭐ The code is available on GitHub ⭐



What's included here?

We’re sharing two Kaggle notebooks that together create a complete inference and submission workflow:
