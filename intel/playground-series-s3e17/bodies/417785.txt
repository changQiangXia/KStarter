Topic #417785: Request for some variety in ongoing challenges
  Author: Ravi Ramakrishnan
  Posted: 2023-06-17 06:53:49.510000
  Votes: 45  Comments: 26

Hello all,

I remember reading a post from the competitions organizer team at the start of the season 3 playground series about the inclusion of non-tabular competitions and a variety of tabular challenges. 
We are currently in the 17th episode and we are yet to witness this variety of challenges insofar. The current tasks involving balanced/ imbalanced classifiers and regression assignments are great and offer a lot of learning experience, but we could consider a few different challenges too for variety. 
The idea of original --&gt; synthetic data is also quite enriching but this could also be altered sometimes to create a variety of tasks. The current set of assignments offer a great experience, but they appear to one-dimensional, covering a small component of tabular ML. 
Over 2022, we experienced 2 time series tasks, 3-4 classifiers, a couple of regressors and a couple of unique challenges (June2022, July2022, Oct2022, Nov2022). Probably replicating such challenges in this year's season may be rewarding and enriching too. 

Some suggestions to make things different- 



Convert a couple of competitions into a code challenge- this will be good practice for featured + code competitions

Include time series and non-tabular challenges in the assignment mix

One may consider closing the discussion and kernel forum for certain period of the assignment. This may encourage individual efforts. I think this could be tested here as an experiment 

Perhaps some assignments could make the original data anonymous. This could be in line with the past 2 year's assignments wherein original datasets were not revealed. 


Thoughts? Comments?
