Target encoding of rows

Just blending alone will probably put you in the top 30, but I had another trick up my sleeve. There are some rows in the train set with the same feature values, even some overlap within the train and test sets. However, these rows do not always have the same labels. So I added two new features: the target encoding (on the train set) and the count of times the row appeared in the train set. For the training, you need to make sure to make your target encoding out of sample, as in this example:

id feature1 feature2 target target_encoding count
0 A 7 1 0.5 2
1 A 7 1 0.5 2
2 A 7 0 1 2

I used CatBoost and ps3e17-eda-ensemble-ml-pipeline-shap for this and applied it only to rows in the test set that have a matching row in the train set.
