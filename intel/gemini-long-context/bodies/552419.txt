Topic #552419: 🏆 Kaggle Long Context Competition - Winners Announcement!
  Author: Paige Bailey
  Posted: 2024-12-19 16:12:24.420000
  Votes: 19  Comments: 33

On behalf of the Gemini Developer and Kaggle Teams and all of our competition organizers, a huge thank you to everyone who participated in the Gemini Long Context Competition! We were blown away by the creativity, ingenuity, and technical skill demonstrated in your submissions. It's clear that the Kaggle community is pushing the boundaries of what's possible with Gemini’s long context capabilities – especially for video understanding and processing, analysis of large codebases, and large-scale text extraction and summarization.

After careful evaluation of all entries, we're thrilled to announce the winners of the Long Context Competition! These projects stood out for their innovative approaches to using large language models to analyze longer context and their potential for real-world impact.

Final Winners:



FrameCut: A Natural Language Video Editor: This project truly impressed us with its comprehensive approach to video editing through natural language, using Gemini’s multimodal longer context capabilities. FrameCut.ai not only demonstrated a strong technical foundation, but also presented a compelling vision for a complete product in just a single month – something I’d definitely use to edit videos!





Exploring Sports Sponsorship – Ads Exposure Analysis: This project utilizes Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash large language model to analyze brand exposure in sports event broadcasts, specifically focusing on F1 racing, an NBA game, and a FIFA World Cup match. The project identifies advertised brands, classifies the type of advertisement (on-site, player, or broadcast), and calculates the total screen time for each brand, providing structured data for visualization. We really loved the novel approach to extracting marketing insights from video content, which could significantly impact how companies evaluate and optimize their sponsorship investments in the multi-billion dollar sports industry.





KeepTrack: Use Gemini to Keep Track of Anything: This project utilizes Gemini's video processing and long context window capabilities to automatically generate a detailed inventory of items from a home video tour. The user simply uploads a video, and the system extracts information on each item, including name, type, description, brand, condition, quantity, estimated worth, and timestamp, outputting the data in a structured CSV format. This is important because it offers a streamlined, accurate solution for cataloging personal belongings, which can significantly simplify the process of obtaining appropriate home and contents insurance and maintaining personal records, potentially saving users both time and money. I would definitely use this myself!





Building Process Documentation from Video with AI: This project showcases how Gemini can be used to automatically generate process documentation from video recordings. We really loved the novel and efficient approach to solving the pervasive problem of inadequate process documentation! This solution would have the potential to deliver significant time and cost savings, enhance operational efficiency, and improve compliance and quality control by automating and simplifying the documentation process.



Congratulations to all the winners!

We were extremely impressed by the overall quality of the submissions. It was truly a challenge to select the winners, as so many projects were deserving of recognition.
