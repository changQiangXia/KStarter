Don't forget to run "Save/run All commit" to attach Gemini 1.5 Flash to your Notebook.

After getting your Gemini API key and add your Secrets to your Notebook, don't forget to choose the "Save and Run All commit".  If you just run a quick version, you won't find any issue on the outcomes. 

However, you won't have any Gemini 1.5 Flash API Model attached to your Notebook yet.  

How i found that? Till now (Oct 19, 2024), we have only 4 users that have the Gemini really attached to their Notebooks. No matter that the others got an API-key on Gemini.

Checking the model Section of this Google - Gemini Long Context (Kaggle Competition)



I realized that I had to run "The Save ALL commit" after reading the Product Feedback: "

"Notebooks that reference the APIs will be automatically “attached” to the relevant model page “Code” tab (you’ll need to create at least one “Save Version”)"
https://www.kaggle.com/discussions/product-feedback/541317
