https://www.kaggle.com/code/adaubas/ps-s3e14-stacking-leastabsolutedeviation-reg

My solution is outlined below.



Feature engineering


I combined train dataset and original dataset.

I added  "fruit_seed" ("fruitset" * "seeds").

I dropped 'RainingDays' and 'AverageOfUpperTRange'.

I used Standard Scaler for all features.

I used target encoding by fruitset during cross validation.

Models


I referred the published notebook below.
https://www.kaggle.com/code/tetsutani/ps3e14-eda-various-models-ensemble-baseline

I used 5 Kfold cross validation.

The following models were ensembled with OptunaWeights.
　   4 * LightGBM, 4 * CatBoost, HistGradientBoosting, RandomForest, Huber, PassiveAggressive and KNeighbors.
