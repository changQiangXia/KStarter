Thank's to Kaggle for this new Episode of Playground Series and thank you to all the players, and thanks to everyone who contributes to interesting and original discussions, notebooks, questions and comments. 😉 to recent comments of @pourchot and @paddykb in notebooks I do not want to refer to, thank you, I do agree.

I've already explained several tricks in two previous discussions :



choice of features, choice of LGBM, decompositions techniques (PLSRegression and PCA) here

LADRegression here to do a blend (last trick : fit_intercept = False)


I just want to add that I had used finally : 



2 CATBoost models where I've put all discrete features as categorical ones (RainindDays and MaxOfUpperTRange)

7 LightGBM models, trained with various sets of features like in https://www.kaggle.com/code/adaubas/ps-s3e14-stacking-leastabsolutedeviation-reg Part 2

Patrick Blackwill @paddykb 's notebook

2 Random Forests : because training them with 'criterion':'absolute_error' option is very slow, I've trained deep RF with 1200 estimators using oob_score=True option by following this scikit-learn example. On Kaggle notebook my RF trained in "only" 10 minutes, and with oob_score=True I was able to get Out Of Bag predictions that I've used like Out Of Fold predictions (first time I did like that). I had not improvement by using ExtraTrees.




I've trained many more models, but it was with this set that I had my best MAE CV score with LADRegression.

I've reached 13th place in only 4 submissions :



first submission : to be sure I wasn't dreaming after reading my CV score : 336.7 at that moment of the competition, with a mean of 8 LGBM models, 336.99 and 2nd place, 6 days before the end of the competition in the Public LB, and 120th in the public LB after the end of competition : what a fire in the public LB last days ! Congratulations to everyone ! 

second submission : to explain LADRegression and to set on fire the Public LB, not only with a blend on test predictions but with explanations and a OOF demonstration (I should have explained with a cross_val_score of LADRegression, but I was so excited). Thank's to @sergiosaharovskiy for your comment about "set LB on fire", it makes me laugh, and congratulations for your result ! You were really impressive in the last days.

the two last ones : my final submissions, with and without @mattop 's post processing trick here. Congratulations for your result !


CV and Public LB were highly reliable, so I worked only with my CV .
