Structure-Based Approach: Optimized Pipeline

If you choose the 3D structure-based graph method, several optimization steps are crucial for competitive performance.

Accelerated MSA Generation

Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA) generation often becomes the bottleneck in structure prediction workflows. GPU-accelerated MMseqs2 solves this challenge by leveraging GPU parallel processing capabilities. The performance gains are substantial: a single NVIDIA L40S GPU delivers 177x speedup compared to JackHMMER, and when integrated with ColabFold, enables 22x faster protein structure prediction compared to AlphaFold2.

Reference: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/boost-alphafold2-protein-structure-prediction-with-gpu-accelerated-mmseqs2/

Optimized Structure Prediction Models

We offer two powerful NIM (NVIDIA Inference Microservice) options for structure prediction:

OpenFold2 NIM: This PyTorch reimplementation maintains AlphaFold2-equivalent accuracy while delivering enhanced speed. It specializes in single-chain protein structure prediction and supports five pre-trained model parameter sets for flexible deployment.

Documentation: https://docs.nvidia.com/nim/bionemo/openfold2/latest/overview.html

Boltz2 NIM: This advanced biomolecular structure prediction service extends beyond proteins to predict RNA, DNA, and molecular complex structures. It also supports ligand binding affinity prediction and constraint-based predictions, making it particularly valuable for drug discovery applications.

Documentation: https://docs.nvidia.com/nim/bionemo/boltz2/latest/overview.html

Accelerated 3D Graph Operations

When working with 3D structure graphs, equivariant layers are essential for maintaining geometric consistency. The cuEquivariance Library optimizes these operations for GPU execution, delivering remarkable performance improvements. The library achieves up great speedup for symmetric contraction operations in popular models like DiffDock and MACE. Specifically designed for geometric neural networks in drug discovery and materials science, cuEquivariance addresses the computational demands of modern equivariant architectures.

References:

  • Doc: https://developer.nvidia.com/cuequivariance
