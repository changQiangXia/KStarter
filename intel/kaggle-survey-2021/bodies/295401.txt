Congratulations everyone!

After evaluating all of your submissions, we are excited to announce the winners of the 2021 Kaggle Machine Learning &amp; Data Science Survey Competition! Thank you to everyone that responded to the survey. We are truly grateful to everyone that shared a public notebook demonstrating how to work with the data. 

First Prize: $10,000 



In the notebook Data Science in 2021: Adaptation or Adoption? @shivamb and @khyeh0719 join the Kaggle Survey dataset with multiple external data sources in order to develop an AI adoption index that is used to better understand the state of data science in 2021.  The authors discuss the maturity of machine learning across different industries and geographies and each discussion is paired with an interactive and engaging data visualization.  Great work and congratulations!



Runners Up: $5,000 each



In the notebook How are the 💃 Ladies and the 🎩 Gents doing, @andradaolteanu uses data from 2017 to 2021 to compare responses from men and women in data science.  The D3.js visualizations are engaging and the 1920's theme is lively and fun.  Great work @andradaolteanu! 


In the notebook Data Scientists &amp; Analysts: What's the difference? @spitfire2nd helps us to understand the differences between Data Analysts and Data Scientists by providing insights in terms of their salary gaps and work responsibilities, while making use of simple yet informative visualization strategies. Great work @spitfire2nd! 


In the notebook Early-Career Kagglers and Skills Development, @katemelianova and @artvolgin compare early-career Master's Degree students to early-career professionals.  The advantages and disadvantages of pursuing graduate education are discussed while also acknowledging that there is no single correct path that is best for everyone.  Great work you two!


In the notebook Head in the Clouds @iamleonie examines trends in cloud computing over time, and includes thoughtful discussions concerning workforce productivity, COVID-19, and geography.  Great work @iamleonie!



Great work everyone!

The Kaggle Team will reach out to the winners with instructions on how to collect their prizes.
