Topic #278727: 8 Popular Storytelling Themes from Past Competitions
  Author: Shivam Bansal
  Posted: 2021-10-15 09:35:12.252000
  Votes: 63  Comments: 14

For the last four years, Kagglers have shared excellent data story notebooks in Kaggle Survey Competitions. Based on my observation, there have been the following nine major themes. Please feel free to suggest any other theme that I missed. Sharing this to help pick the topic of your analysis: 

1. Geographical Analysis: A story about AI, ML, and Data Science in a particular region - People have shared analysis on how the trends are different by their region of interest from the rest of the other regions. Previous winners' notebooks also include great AI stories from Japan and Africa. 
Examples: Link1 | Link2 

2. Gender Analysis: A very important topic in which people have analyzed a particular gender's representation in machine learning and data science space across different categories - education levels, job levels,  salaries, etc. Previous winning notebooks include very interesting analyses on the gender divide and women's participation. 
Examples: Link1 | Link2 

3. Emergence of Tech: This theme involves the analysis of popular, interesting, and rising technologies such as - the evolution of interest in AutoML, or DeepLearning insights etc.
Examples: Link1 | Link

4. Programming Languages: This great battle of Python vs R has uncovered some of the engaging discussions and insights. Some interesting analysis includes a comparison of the typical profile of an R user differs from a Python user or changes in percentage of users over years.
Examples: Link1 | Link2

5. Education Levels: Many winning notebooks had a comprehensive analysis of education levels and data professionals. This includes the analysis in terms of software, hardware, technologies, etc.    
Examples: Link1 | Link2

6. Time Series Comparative Insights: Kaggle has beautifully organized and maintain the survey datasets over the years, users have compared how the trends/insights have changed over the years. An example is - change in average salaries of data scientists over time across geographies. 
Examples: Link1 | Link2

7. Valuable Data Scientist: Kaggle survey dataset contains valuable information about different groups of profiles - people working in big or small, people earning lower or higher than average etc. This type of data has been used to answer questions such as - how to become more valuable. 
Examples: Link1 | Link2

8. Become a Data Scientist: Valuable, and important tips with compelling stats on how to become a data scientist with insights on technologies to learn, what major's to choose etc. 
Examples: Link1 | Link2
