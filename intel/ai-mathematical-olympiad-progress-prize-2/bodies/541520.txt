0.5 is given when the LLM model generates all reasoning steps correct, but makes silly one (and only one) arithmetic error like summation. These can be easily corrected by running verifying python code.


"artificial reasoning" is hypotheses steps generation + verification. 




objectives

It’s time to level up! For coming 5 months of battling, here are my key objectives for this competition that I’d like to share with my fellow Kagglers:

1. Use SOTA Models for Math Reasoning at Olympiad Level:



Collect public datasets.

Reproduce evaluation results.

Test models on Kaggle and my custom data.

Analyze weaknesses and predict final score limits for AIMO-2.

Learn how generate and more importantly, how to sample and use LLM synthetic generated data


2. Implement Top Reasoning Algorithms:



Explore CoT, self-play, new RL methods and others from leading papers.- 

DeepMind AlphaGeometry.

CoT with stepwise math symbolic programming.


3. Scaling law of LLMs:



experiment on quantization.

Efficient inference for large models (72B).


4. LLM for Kaggle



Leverage ChatGPT-01 Preview for generate new ideas and plan competition strategies.
