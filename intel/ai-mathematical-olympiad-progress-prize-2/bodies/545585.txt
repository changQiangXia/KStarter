Topic #545585: Prompting Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct to check their own solution

Current solutions to AIMO involves generating a batch of solutions, exact the answers, and nominate the answer usually by majority voting.

To get more reliable results from majority voting you either



increase the number of correct answers, or

decrease the number of incorrect answers


One strategy to decrease the number of incorrect answers is to get the model to check their own answer.

This is the prompt that I use

Start with the final answer, and go through your solution in reverse to find the contradiction. Print the contradiction in \boxed{}.


These are some design details of the prompt I want to elaborate



go through your solution in reverse - I do not want to model to repeat their solution, and likely reaching the same wrong answer.

Start with the final answer - I want to further ensure that the response actually starts from the end.

Print the contradiction in \boxed{} - It seems that Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct strongly prefers to return the result in \boxed{}. If I don't do this, I often see Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct surfacing the contradiction and then returning the same answer.


To check whether has the model surfaced a contradiction, I check the contents in \boxed{}. I consider a contradiction to be found if any boxed content satisfies all of the following conditions



is not empty

is not the initial answer

does not contain "no contradiction" in any case


You can see the results in Version 43 of my notebook, which scored 6 on the public leaderboard.

I am curious if anyone has a better prompt to get their model to check their solutions.
