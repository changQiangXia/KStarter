FAQ



Why are you building this? I find myself refreshing the leaderboard / submissions page to estimate how long my last submission is taking. It is 2025, this should have been automated.

What is missing from the Kaggle leaderboard? You do not see how long the latest submission is taking. You only know how long at least the last submission is taking.

What are all the dashes? The score is a dash if we do not know how much the latest submission has scored. The public knows the exact score of the latest submission only if the latest submission is the best submission. I do not have data before December 12.

Why are there runtime longer than 5 hours? According to the rules "Submission runtimes have been obfuscated. If you repeat the exact same submission you will see up to 30 minutes of variance in the time before you receive your score.".

Why are there super-short runtimes? It is very likely that the submission has failed.
