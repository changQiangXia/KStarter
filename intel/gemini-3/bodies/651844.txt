The Vibe Code with Gemini 3 Pro in AI Studio competition is officially live!

For the next week, you can use the free tier in Google AI Studio to build with Gemini 3 Pro. This is our latest state-of-the-art model that uses advanced reasoning to understand your intent and solve complex problems.

With the Build feature in AI Studio, you can generate AI-powered applications in minutes.

Our challenge to you: Move beyond simple scripts and use this reasoning engine to build something with a real "wow" factor. Create an application that solves a hard problem or creates a delightful new experience.

Whether you want to tackle a global challenge in Science or Business, fix a personal daily frustration, or just build something that makes the internet more fun, if it has impact, we want to see it.

We can't wait to see the amazing applications you build!
