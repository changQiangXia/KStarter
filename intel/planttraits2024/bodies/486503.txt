Hello Everyone,

Firstly, we would like to express our gratitude for your participation. 

Recently, it has come to our attention that some participants have utilized the sample_submission.csv file to enhance their scores on the leaderboard. In order to ensure fairness, we have made updates to the test dataset that includes test images and test.csv. It's important to note that no changes have been made to the training dataset. Therefore, you can create a new submission by making inferences on the updated test dataset without incurring any additional training costs. We believe this solution effectively addresses the issue.

Furthermore, discussions have arisen regarding the leaderboard still displaying previous scores. To rectify this, we have decided to reset the leaderboard. Given that the deadline is still more than two months away, this reset will provide ample time for all participants to make their submissions. We are already in touch with the Kaggle team to make it feasible, so this might be effective this week. We will update you once it is done.
EDIT - The leaderboard is now updated

Thank you for your understanding, and we sincerely appreciate your cooperation in ensuring a fair and competitive environment for all participants.
