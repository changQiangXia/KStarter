Hi all,

I created an easy-to-use 3D viewer for this competition's data volumes. It



Displays the labeled regions, optionally overlaid on the papyrus sheets.

Finds connected regions by region-growing and draws them in a single color, making it easy to spot sheets that are touching.

Requires no installation; runs locally in your browser. (Uses WebGL, so a GPU is required)

Feel free to try it out, the project is on github, here. I hope you find it useful!
