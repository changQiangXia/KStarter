This time I want to gain some intuitions on some feature engineering and share with you to start some discussion. It is emphasized that visualizing the features can be a valuable step in the feature engineering process as it can reveal patterns that can be exploited by the model to improve its predictions.

Some ideas to try:

  1. Calculate the total acidity of a wine -> fixed acidity + volatile acidity + citric acid
  2. Determine the percentage of density that is attributed to acid -> total acidity / density
  3. Determine the percentage of density that is attributed to alcohol -> density * alcohol
  4. Determine the percentage of density that comes from sulphates -> sulphates / density
  5. Calculate the ratio of sulphates to volatile acidity as it has been observed that a high ratio results in better wine quality -> sulphates / volatile acidity
  6. Same behavior of (5) applies to sulphates - chlorides ratio -> sulphates / chlorines.
  7. Multiply sulphates and alcohol content as both features have similar correlation coefficients with the target variable, and by combining them we may amplify the correlations -> sulphates * alcohol.
