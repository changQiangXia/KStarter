Hi all,

This time I want to gain some intuitions on some feature engineering and share with you to start some discussion. It is emphasized that visualizing the features can be a valuable step in the feature engineering process as it can reveal patterns that can be exploited by the model to improve its predictions.

Some ideas to try:



Calculate the total acidity of a wine  -&gt; fixed acidity + volatile acidity + citric acid

Determine the percentage of density that is attributed to acid -&gt; total acidity / density

Determine the percentage of density that is attributed to alcohol -&gt; density * alcohol

Determine the percentage of density that comes from sulphates -&gt; sulphates / density

Calculate the ratio of sulphates to volatile acidity as it has been observed that a high ratio results in better wine quality -&gt; sulphates / volatile acidity

Same behavior of (5) applies to sulphates - chlorides ratio -&gt; sulphates / chlorines.

Multiply sulphates and alcohol content as both features have similar correlation coefficients with the target variable, and by combining them we may amplify the correlations -&gt; sulphates * alcohol.


The code:

train_df[] = train_df[] + train_df[] + train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[]  / train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[]  * train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[]  / train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[] / train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[] / train_df[]
train_df[] = train_df[] * train_df[]
