First of all, thank you to the hosts for organizing such an exciting competition. We had a great time building PTCG agents.

Full details will be published once the strategy division is over.


Summary



Pure multi-agent self-play RL (PPO)

Three-stage curriculum: multi-deck model → archetype-specific models → deck-specific models


Training Pipeline



Collect top-rated and widely-played decks from the LB to build a deck pool

Multi-deck model: self-play PPO with both players' decks randomly sampled
from the pool

Archetype-specific models: fix our agent's deck archetype and randomly sample the
opponent's deck from the pool

Deck-specific models: fix our agent to a single deck and randomly
sample the opponent's deck from the pool



Selected Decks



Slowking

Ogerpon + Meganium


Modeling



Entity Transformer (~12M parameters)


Submission Agent Logic



To be updated


Training Environment



C++ vectorized environment (end-to-end training throughput was ~30 games/sec on an RTX 3090 + 16-core CPU)


Results

ongoing); we expect the final score to land in the 1100–1160 range
