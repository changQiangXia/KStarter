Summary

  • Pure multi-agent self-play RL (PPO)
  • Three-stage curriculum: multi-deck model → archetype-specific models → deck-specific models

Training Pipeline

  1. Collect top-rated and widely-played decks from the LB to build a deck pool
  2. Multi-deck model: self-play PPO with both players' decks randomly sampled from the pool
  3. Archetype-specific models: fix our agent's deck archetype and randomly sample the opponent's deck from the pool
  4. Deck-specific models: fix our agent to a single deck and randomly sample the opponent's deck from the pool
