I have initiated the discussion during the previous competition of why small test dataset can eventually be harmfull for all participants. Now we have bots probing the leaderboard.

It seems like an useless attempt to do it since there are no prizes (except kaggle swags) but risks are great…
Though, Playground Series attracts a lot of people every two weeks and we saw a growth of really bright kagglers during the time. A prestige of getting high on the leaderboard (winning eventually) and social capital cannot be ignored.

Additionally to the above, having fewer samples in test, means that simple probing might help to increase the dataset for the training part.
