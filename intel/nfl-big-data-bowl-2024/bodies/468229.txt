Hi all,

Thanks to everyone for participating in our competition. A few highlights:



We set a record for our analytics competition with roughly 300 submissions, a 15% bump over last year

Metric track continued to be our most popular -- something like 45/30/25 split between metric/undergrad/coaching 

We're sending off papers to team analytics staffers who do all the grading. Given the number of submissions, please allow a few weeks -- we hope to have finalists picked by early February

Some key take homes from Andrew Patton of the NFL league office here: https://twitter.com/anpatt7/status/1746209633562443948


I'd encourage folks to share their submission on the resumes, LinkedIn profiles, and on social media (#BigDataBowl). It's a great chance to make your way into sports analytics, or general careers in data.


Thanks again,

Big Data Bowl organizing crew
