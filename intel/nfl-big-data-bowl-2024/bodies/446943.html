The National Football League is back with our sixth Big Data Bowl!

This year's competition explores the role of tackling -- that is, when a ball carrier has the football and is matriculating his way down the field, how does the defense bring him down?

If you are struggling for ideas, our overview page provides several tackling themes to consider. But feel free to play around with the data, meet and exchange ideas with football or data science experts, and learn (and tell us!) the secret sauce behind tackling.

For folks wanting a job in sports analytics, consider this your opportunity/resume builder. And if you are new to the field, welcome! We're excited to follow along over the next few months, and if you have any questions about the themes, data set, or grading process, feel free to ping us here!
