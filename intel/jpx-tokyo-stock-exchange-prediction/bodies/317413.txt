Topic #317413: Ongoing leaderboard redactions
  Author: Sohier Dane
  Posted: 2022-04-07 00:23:15.891000
  Votes: 116  Comments: 78

As noted on the data tab, it is straightforward to use historic data to get a perfect score given that stock prices are public information. Making perfect submissions is not against the rules but does degrade the utility of the leaderboard for other competitors. We're going to experiment with removing submissions on a regular basis if they would not be feasible without historic data. For now I will try to get to this once a week.

My hope is that this makes it more interesting to post submissions that are more in line with the spirit of the competition. Please post any feedback you have about this policy in this thread; we will be keeping an eye out to see if this approach is worth repeating in the future.

Edit: I'm going to halt the redactions for the remainder of the competition to ensure everyone can use models trained on the entire dataset for their final submission.
