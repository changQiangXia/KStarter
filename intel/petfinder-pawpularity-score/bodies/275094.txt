model: swin_transformer


transformer is better than cnn.

image_size: 224

data: pet-centric cropped dataset (not important)

treat regression problem as classification


we can treat regression as a classification by normalizing the target to the range [0, 1] and using xentropy loss. this method is used by elo competition 1st place and avito competition 3rd place. classification is better than regression for me.
