I am really surprised (and pleased) by my result.

My model is a single 10 fold swin large transformer. The main difference with public notebooks is that I used an ordinal regression head https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_regression

I didn't use fastai either, just plain pytorch.

I read that bce was giving great results but I couldn't agree to that, hence I went my regression head despite the poor public LB,

My CV was 17.25. CV score is computer with RMSE on out of fold predictions. It is not the average of fold RMSE. The latter is a bit optimistic.

Edit, A little more details. I shared my submission notebook, https://www.kaggle.com/cpmpml/pet-064 . I didn't clean it, it was done in a hurry.

There are two checkpoint for each fold model.

The first checkpoints are from training on this competition data (pet_052 checkpoints)

The second checkpoints (pet_064) are from further training the same model with pseudo labels on previous petfinder competition training data. I had big hopes but they proved to be wrong. The improvement is tiny.

I had three subs:

  • pet_52 checkpoints

  • pet_64 checkpoints

  • both checkpoints.

Their scores are very similar. Their CV was also very similar.
