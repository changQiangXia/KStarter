Hi everyone! I just submitted my benchmark for the Metacognition track. It tests 5 distinct aspects of metacognition across 3 frontier models (Gemini 2.5 Flash, Claude Haiku 4.5, DeepSeek V3.2). Key finding: all 3 models share the same weakness — they cannot predict their own difficulty before answering. T4 and T5 both PASS across all models, but T1, T2, T3 show consistent failures. Novel metric: Calibration Fragility Index (CFI) — measures how much confidence breaks under deceptive questions.
