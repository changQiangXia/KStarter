Here are some feature engineering ideas that might be useful:

  1. Ratios:
    Revenue per unit: store_sales / unit_sales
    Sales per sqft: store_sales / Store_sqft
    Units per sqft: unit_sales / Store_sqft
    Children ratio: Total_children / Num_children_at_home
    Weight per unit: Gross_weight / unit_sales
  2. Polynomials:
    Create polynomial features for numerical columns such as store_sales, unit_sales, Total_children, avg_cars_at_home, Num_children_at_home, Gross_weight, Units_per_case, and Store_sqft to capture potential non-linearities.
  3. Interactions:
    Multiply binary features (Coffee_bar * Video_store) to capture potential interactions between store amenities.
    Combine numerical features (store_sales * Store_sqft or Total_children * Num_children_at_home) to capture additional interactions between them.
  4. Aggregates:
    Create aggregated features using store_sales, unit_sales, Total_children, and Num_children_at_home, such as mean, median, or sum for each group of stores with similar characteristics (stores with similar store_sales or store_sqft ranges).
  5. Bins:
    Bin numerical columns such as store_sales, unit_sales, Total_children, avg_cars_at_home, Num_children_at_home, Gross_weight, Units_per_case, and Store_sqft. Play around with bin size and create larger categories like low, medium, and high sales, this will reduce the cardinality of these features and potentially some of the noise.

Feel free to add in the comments any additional ideas you have!
