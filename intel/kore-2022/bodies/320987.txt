I produced an animation of a game replay.



These are the current features in the animation.



Color indicates the player

Crosshairs refers to a shipyard


The top left number indicates the number of ships stationed

The bottom right indicates the

Triangle refers to a deployed fleet


The top left number indicates the number of ships in the fleet

The bottom left number indicates the amount of Kore carried by the fleet

The lines represent the flight plan. X in the flight plan is the build instruction.

The number in the field represents the amount of Kore


You can produce the animation with any of the following



A match replay python object generated from a gameplay between two agents

A match replay file downloaded with robga's notebook

A match replay file given its match id, for example this


On my notebook, you can use the slider to look at the exact turn you are interested in.

Some improvements I may make over time, feel free to add on or concur

I hope this helps to make sense of the game and the strategies played.
