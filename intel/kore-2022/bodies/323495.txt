Hi there!

Recently I've been looking at ways to make it easier to debug match replays. Although the official visualizer and the visualizer by Tong Hui Kang work well, they are both missing features that I think to be useful, like an interactive board, player logs, and a way to easily see which shipyard each action is played by.

To fulfill my own wishes, and potentially the wishes of other fellow participants, I created my own visualizer called Koreye 2022. Most of its features can be seen in the screenshot below:


Koreye 2022 is available at https://jmerle.github.io/koreye-2022/ and its source code is available at https://github.com/jmerle/koreye-2022.

Have fun :)
