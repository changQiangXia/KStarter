Additional Context



loss is MAE.

MLM on training data for 15 epochs,  max_length is 1024. It takes 3 days. 

training for 10 epochs, max_length is 2048. It takes 7 days. I think set max_length to 5120 will greatly improve model performance but I have not enough A100~

inference: max_length is 5120. It takes 6 hours.

I used 1 * A100(80GB)  for the last two weeks and 1 * 3090(24GB) at the early stage.
