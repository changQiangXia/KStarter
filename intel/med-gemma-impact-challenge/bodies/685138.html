Congratulations to all the winners of the MedGemma Impact Challenge. The projects selected are genuinely impressive and reflect the potential of HAI-DEF models in real healthcare settings.

That said, many of us invested months of unpaid work into our submissions, and I'd like to respectfully ask whether the organizers could share individual scoring breakdowns or at minimum a general rubric with scores per criterion for non-winning entries.

This isn't just about personal feedback. It's also about transparency. When a competition is judged internally by the model's own creators, participants reasonably want to understand how decisions were made. Structured feedback would go a long way toward demonstrating that the process was fair and rigorous, and would help the community build better submissions in future challenges.

Thank you for the opportunity. I hope this kind of feedback becomes a standard part of future HAI-DEF competitions.
