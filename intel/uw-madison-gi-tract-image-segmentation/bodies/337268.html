Our single-model performance was not very good, and for a long time we even lost the confidence to take a gold. But surprisingly it worked very well after ensemble.

So what I mainly want to share here is how we can lift the ensemble performance by making diversity.

Summary

  • 5 Fold cross validation by group spliting case
  • 2.5D Models (x, y, z 3 types in total)
  • 3D Models
  • Post-processing

2.5D Models

Used library:

  • https://github.com/qubvel/segmentation_models.pytorch
  • https://github.com/rwightman/pytorch-image-models
  • https://github.com/sithu31296/semantic-segmentation

We first constructed the dataset into 3D format, and then by cutting the x, y, and z axes separately, we can obtain 3 different types of 2.5D datasets. I'll call it 2.5Dx (original), 2.5Dy, 2.5Dz.

Then we trained 9 models on those 2.5D datasets:

  • efficientnet v2s unet on 2.5Dx dataset

  • nfnet l0 unet on 2.5Dx dataset

  • convnext small unet on 2.5Dx dataset

  • pvtv2b2 segformer on 2.5Dx dataset

  • mitb2 segformer on 2.5Dx dataset

  • efficientnet v2s unet on 2.5Dy dataset

  • convnext small unet++ on 2.5Dy dataset

  • efficientnet v2s unet on 2.5Dz dataset

  • convnext small unet++ on 2.5Dz dataset

3D Models

Used library:

  • https://github.com/qubvel/segmentation_models.pytorch
  • https://github.com/rwightman/pytorch-image-models
  • https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI

In this part I believe that we do not do anything different from what most people do.
we trained :

  • resnet unet on 3D dataset
  • efficientnet v2s unet on 3D dataset
  • efficientnet v2m unet on 3D dataset

The highest pub LB score of a single model mentioned above is only 0.875
For the final ensemble we simply averaged the output of all models then use threshold 0.3 we get pub LB 0.889

Post-processing

For those images with a predicted area less than 50 pixels, we discarded them. By using this we get pub LB 0.890
