The Dataset Description for this competition contains the following comment regarding the training labels: "Please note the presence of a small number of files labeled -1. Physicists are currently unable to determine the status of these files."

Well, I think their status can be updated now, to "easter egg!"

If you plot their signal magnitudes as 2D arrays, you'll see the following:


50f09e37e appears to be a cartoon of two black holes about to collide:



62b0dd011 is the image that was broadcast into the universe in 1974, as part of the SETI program:



And b7666b451 shows the 2017 physics Nobel Prize winners (for the discovery of gravitational waves), Rainer Weiss, Barry Barish and Kip Thorne:




(On the other hand, if these are really signals received by LIGO, we all may have to re-think our views on extraterrestrial intelligence!)
