Hello everyone, and welcome to the BigQuery AI Hackathon!

BigQuery continues to push the boundaries of what a data warehouse can do by adding generative AI, vector search, and multimodal capabilities. Now you can query, search, and join your structured data with unstructured data in a single SQL query!

Our challenge to you is to also push the boundaries and envision how BigQuery AI can help you build ground-breaking solutions to real-world business problems.

While there are three distinct approaches in this hackathon, we encourage you to be creative and even combine approaches if it makes sense.

If you're new to BigQuery AI, the "Getting Started" section on the competition page has notebooks, videos, and blogs to help you dive in. This forum is your space to connect, ask questions, and collaborate.

We can't wait to see the innovative solutions you build. Your work will provide valuable feedback on BigQuery AI features.

Good luck, and happy hacking!
