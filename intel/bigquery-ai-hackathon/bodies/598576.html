Explore the many ways below to utilize BigQuery AI through free tiers and provided credits:

90-day, $300 Free Trial: New Google Cloud users can take advantage of a 90-day trial period that includes $300 in free Cloud Billing credits to explore and evaluate Google Cloud products and services (including BigQuery).

BigQuery Free Tier - All Google Cloud customers can use select Google Cloud products—like BigQuery—free of charge, within specified monthly usage limits. When you stay within the Free Tier limits, these resources are not charged against your Free Trial credits or to your Cloud Billing account's payment method after your trial ends.

Hackathon-provided Credits - If you have used the free trial already, and you've exceeded Free Tier limits, fill out this two-question form to let the host team know about your progress so far, and they'll provide you with an additional $50 credit to support your efforts in this Hackathon. Additional credits will be distributed approximately weekly while supplies last.

Instrumentless Credits - if you do not have access to a credit card, fill out this three-question form to let the host team know about your progress so far. You will be provided multiple links to redeem $5 instrumentless credits. Please be aware that you must be able to maintain and keep track of the credits or you may lose access to the project.
