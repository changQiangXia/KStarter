Welcome to the Big Data Derby! We're so excited to once again expand our Analytics Competition offerings to the Kaggle community to explore unique competition ideas that tell compelling stories. 

If this is your first analytics competition, I'd encourage you to see Kaggle Grandmaster John Miller's excellent write-up on tips and tricks for making a great analytics submission, as well as the top solutions of other analytics competitions.

The types of questions the NYTHRA/NYRA is looking to see examined are provided on the Evaluation page. Please note that list is not comprehensive, nor is it meant to be a guide for you to try and specifically answer (or address all the questions). 

As a reminder - Submissions must be made public at the competition deadline to be considered eligible for scoring (The NYTHA/NYRA has to be able to actually see your hard work!).

Best of luck, and happy modeling!
