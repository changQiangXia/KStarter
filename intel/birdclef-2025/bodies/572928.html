BirdCLEF: Summary of Techniques from Past Top Solutions (2024)

This table summarizes key techniques and approaches observed in the write-ups of the top 10 solutions from a recent BirdCLEF competition (likely 2024, based on common themes like pseudo-labeling). It aims to provide insights and potential starting points for fellow Kagglers tackling BirdCLEF 2025.

Disclaimer: This is based on summaries and may not capture every nuance. Always refer to the original write-ups for full details.

Key Takeaways

  • Domain Shift is Real: Bridging the gap between training data and noisy soundscapes (often via pseudo-labeling) was critical.
  • EfficientNets Rule (esp. B0): A strong and efficient default choice, especially considering inference constraints.
  • Ensembling is Non-Negotiable: Combining diverse models consistently improves results. Diversity can come from different backbones, input features, training data/folds, or hyperparameters.
  • Temporal Context Matters: Smoothing predictions over time is a simple but effective post-processing step.
  • Inference Speed is Key: Optimizing models (OpenVINO/ONNX, quantization) is necessary for the competition format.
