as explained in the original data :https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/semustafacevik/software-defect-prediction
Number of attributes: 22 (5 different lines of code measure, 
 3 McCabe metrics, 4 base Halstead measures, 8 derived 
 Halstead measures, a branch-count, and 1 goal field)

Attribute Information:

  . loc             :  % McCabe line count  code
  . v(g)            :  % McCabe 
  . ev(g)           :  % McCabe 
  . iv(g)           :  % McCabe 
  . n               :  % Halstead total operators + operands
  . v               :  % Halstead 
  . l               :  % Halstead 
  . d               :  % Halstead 
  . i               :  % Halstead 
 . e               :  % Halstead 
 . b               :  % Halstead 
 . t               :  % Halstead time estimator
 . lOCode          :  % Halstead line count
 . lOComment       :  % Halstead count  lines  comments
 . lOBlank         :  % Halstead count  blank lines
 . lOCodeAndComment: numeric
 . uniq_Op         :  % unique operators
 . uniq_Opnd       :  % unique operands
 . total_Op        :  % total operators
 . total_Opnd      :  % total operands
 : branchCount     :  %  the flow graph
 . defects         : {,} % module has/has  one  more  reported defects
