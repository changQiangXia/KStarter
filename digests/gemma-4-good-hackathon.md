### Welcome to the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon!

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-good-hackathon/discussion/687467

Hi Kagglers,

My name is Glenn, and I’m a PMM on the Gemma team. I’ve been part of this journey since the very first Gemma release, and I’m constantly blown away by the Gemmaverse this community has created. You’ve proven that open models can truly be a force for good.

As a judge, I’m looking for projects with technical depth, but I also want to help your work reach as many people as possible. A powerful video is the best way to ensure your solution gets the attention it deserves. While Video Pitch & Storytelling is 30% of your score, its real value is in helping the world understand the impact of what 
you've built. Here are three examples of past submissions that did this brilliantly: 

1. Assistive Tech for the Visually Impaired

Why it stood out: This project was deeply personal. A developer built a device to help his blind brother navigate the world. It perfectly captures the spirit of the Health & Sciences and Digital Equity tracks by solving a real-world accessibility challenge with empathy.

2. Skin Health Tracking

Why it stood out: This is a masterclass in a demo video. The developer is on camera, clearly explaining the impact, and showing the app in action. It’s professional, grounded, and clearly demonstrates the real-world utility we look for in the Health & Sciences track.

3. Voice-Controlled Computing

Why it stood out: While not a Gemma project, the showmanship here stands out in my mind as incredible. The use case was forward-thinking and the execution was seamless. It's a great example of the "wow" factor. Fun fact: The developer behind this is now an intern at Google DeepMind!

My Advice for Your Submission:

Talk to real users: If possible, interview the people you're building for. Their feedback will make your solution more practical and your story much more authentic.

Tell a story: Don't just show the code; show us the person whose life is changed by your solution.

Show, don't just tell: Use your 3-minute video to prove your project is functional and impactful.

I can't wait to see how you use Gemma 4 to help the world. Happy hacking!

Glenn

Hi everyone,

First and foremost, a massive thank you to everyone who participated in The Gemma 4 Good Hackathon. We are blown away by the response and the dedication of this community.

We wanted to give you a quick update on where we are in the judging process:

We received well over 1,500 submissions! Waaaay more than our last one.

Our team is actively working on reviewing all of the entries right now. Because of the sheer volume of submissions, we don't have an exact timeline for when the final winners will be announced. We want to make sure every single project gets the thorough attention it deserves, but please know we are doing our absolute best to move through them as quickly as possible.

From what we've seen so far, there are some truly amazing projects. We are so incredibly thankful to see so many of you harnessing Gemma to drive positive change with global impact.

Thank you for your patience as we work through this massive (and inspiring) list of projects. We will keep you updated here in the forums as soon as we have more concrete dates to share.

Glenn on behalf of the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon Team
