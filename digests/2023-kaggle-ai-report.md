### Discussion about points and medals for this competition

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/2023-kaggle-ai-report/discussion/409784

As there was already a discussion about this on Twitter and others have expressed similar concerns, it might make sense to discuss this topic here on Kaggle - specifically as early as possible.

First of all, I find the idea of this competition very cool. A lot of great ideas get first developed and researched on Kaggle, and there is sometimes a bit of a lack of public perception of these. So collecting and publishing them sounds like a good thing to do.

However, reviewing these will always need to follow some sort of typical research peer process. This competition is following a similar direction, by having peer review (fellow kagglers) and expert assessment (program chairs, GMs). 

However, one of my reasons why I started Kaggling was to exactly move away from this subjective process. I always felt particularly intrigued by the fully objective metric-based assessment of my submissions. There is no human intervention when it is decided who is on top or not.

Now with awarding medals and points to this competition, I feel concerned about the integrity of the competition tier. Top Kagglers have spent countless hours of their life to work on competitive objective metric-based competitions and being judged by other kagglers, who you are actually competing with on the global leaderboard, feels a bit unnerving to me.

I know that there are different opinions about this, so happy to hear what others think. By the way, I think Kaggle should start their own scientific journal where people can submit and publish their work.
