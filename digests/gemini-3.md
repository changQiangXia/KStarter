### Welcome to the Vibe Code with Gemini 3 Pro Hackathon

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemini-3/discussion/651844

Hello Kagglers,

The Vibe Code with Gemini 3 Pro in AI Studio competition is officially live!

For the next week, you can use the free tier in Google AI Studio to build with Gemini 3 Pro. This is our latest state-of-the-art model that uses advanced reasoning to understand your intent and solve complex problems.

With the Build feature in AI Studio, you can generate AI-powered applications in minutes.

Our challenge to you: Move beyond simple scripts and use this reasoning engine to build something with a real "wow" factor. Create an application that solves a hard problem or creates a delightful new experience.

Whether you want to tackle a global challenge in Science or Business, fix a personal daily frustration, or just build something that makes the internet more fun, if it has impact, we want to see it.

We can't wait to see the amazing applications you build!

The Google DeepMind Team

### Magnificent Gemini3 Pro Write-ups. They're worth it. Hosts will suffer to read and decide from all this 4,100. 

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemini-3/discussion/662567

Firstly, the list of Write-ups is huge (205 pages, each one with 20 Write-ups?)

I started to read the Write-ups since the competition ended yesterday. Works and videos are amazing.

Forget the Code section, many Notebooks I wasn't even able to get how to apply them, no matter how good is their code.

From a non-coder perspective the ideas are brilliant. As their presentation quality too. 

The six judges will face a huge task to decide which of the 4,100 submissions will take the 50 prizes.

For the competitors, remember if you aren't among the 50th (money zone) the opportunity to have your work been seen in such a challenge is unique.

You All are worth it.

Best wishes,

Marília Prata.
