### Lux AI Challenge 2022 Beta - What to Expect and Resources

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/lux-ai-2022-beta/discussion/363366

Welcome to the beta phase of Lux AI 2022!

As mentioned by Addison, we will be using this beta phase to help test the environment early with public feedback and competition. Based on feedback we will balance out the environment, and potentially change some mechanics if necessary in order to encourage a fun experience for all with lots of room to explore.

Throughout the beta period, our team at The Lux AI Challenge will be developing a lot of things that will be missing in the current beta

More custom assets and improved web visualizer (both on Kaggle and on our website)

Story-based events

Better tooling

More starter kits

Resources

Everything you need can be found on the main GitHub Repository, including starter kits, API documentation, and how to run episodes and test/evaluate locally. We highly recommend reading through our docs to get a sense of this year's environment as it's quite a bit different! The specifications are here: https://www.lux-ai.org/specs-2022-beta

A python-based Jupyter notebook style tutorial can be found here: https://www.kaggle.com/code/stonet2000/lux-ai-season-2-jupyter-notebook-tutorial

Some notable changes to be aware of

There are two distinct phases to the game (and two action spaces)

Mobile Units / Robots are not given single actions but action queues, a sequence of discrete actions to move, dig, transfer etc. 

Robots come in two weight classes, light and heavy, each with its own pros and cons.

Unit collisions are enabled and have a collision resolution ruleset dependent on the robot's weight class.

We want to build an awesome community around AI competitions like these and we highly recommend joining our Lux AI community discord. There you can live chat with other competitors, discuss strategies, share open-source tools, and more!

Remember that Kaggle and the Lux AI community are for everyone. Whether you're teaming up or sharing tips, we expect everyone to follow the Community Guidelines

Make sure to also follow our socials 😊

### Upcoming Changes

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/lux-ai-2022-beta/discussion/365579

Hey all,

Thanks for participating in the beta! We've fixed a huge amount of bugs and received some really awesome feedback - keep it coming!

We are considering a number of changes, but here are the ones we are working on right now.

Action queues wait

Instead of failing the next action if there isn't enough power, the robot will instead wait until it has enough power to execute the next action. For example if you give [unit.dig(), unit.dig()] to a robot with 0 power it will wait until it has enough energy to dig, then dig, then against wait until it has enough energy to dig, then dig. 

This change should make it easier to use action queues.

Bidding, map and factory placement changes.

While adding an additional factory allowed for more asymmetric gameplay, we like this proposal better:

change the map to asymmetric

bid to see factory placement order

alternate factory placement

factories can now be placed anywhere on the map (no more assigned sides)

you cannot place a factory within X units of an already-placed factory.

each player has the same amount of factories

Since it might be advantageous to first or second depending on the map bids will take the form (desired_placement, amount) (eg: (0, 50) for going first bidding 50) with the winner being the one with the largest amount. Tie break would be random or based on player id.

This change will allow for more interaction between agents and make it less devastating if you don't have a solid bidding strategy.

Note: This will be a breaking change!

Please let us know your thoughts and feedback on these proposed changes! We are also considering changes to action queues, I'll update here once we get that finalized.

Best,

Bovard

### Welcome to Lux AI 2022 - Beta

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/lux-ai-2022-beta/discussion/362825

Hi All,

We're excited to launch the next season of Lux AI soon, but before we do, we need your help! We're launching it in a preview state early to help with gameplay/rule balancing and establishing a fair and fun competition, soon to be launched. As such, this competition does not have cash prizes, points, or medals - but we hope to gain your feedback for when the featured competition goes live! Feel free to posts any bugs you find, or questions/concerns in this thread.

We hope to have this competition up for just a few short weeks so we can polish our configuration and launch the full, featured competition soon thereafter.

### Preview: Limiting active submissions

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/lux-ai-2022-beta/discussion/363479

We'll be trying out a new feature on this competition to limit the number of active submissions. As you submit new (successful) submissions, your old submissions will start to be disabled. Currently the setting is for 3 active submissions.

This will allow for a faster game rate and more games on the submissions you care about!

Please let me know your thoughts/comments/concerns!
