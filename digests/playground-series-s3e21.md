### 66th Place Solution for Playground 3.21 "Improve a Fixed Model the Data-Centric Way"

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s3e21/discussion/438609

It is safe to say that this competition has been very different from most other playground series competitions. It was also really tough. My second best submission was resubmitting the raw competition dataset. 

My best approach, outlined here, is based on censoring and reimputing data. Many variables had obviously erroneous data values, and by censoring these values and reimputing them, the hope was to get a more realistic looking dataset with which to train the model.

Context section

Business context: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s3e21

Data context: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s3e21/data

Overview of the approach

Compared to the original dataset, we notice that there are a lot of strange looking data values in almost all columns. Some background research gives typical ranges of the variables. For example, dissolved oxygen levels of freshwater could typically range between 5-14 mgO2/cub. dm. Less than 4 is possible but not (ecologically) desirable, and zero is the absolute minimum of this variable. Dissolved oxygen levels can theoretically be larger than 14 temporarily, but open water will quickly lose any supersaturated oxygen to the atmosphere. So, a physically plausible range for the DO columns is 0–20. Similar research can provide typical ranges for the other variables. 

In light of this, one approach could be to remove rows where variables fall outside specified physically realistic ranges. A more automated machine-learning approach to this is to have the ranges of variables as hyperparameters of a (preprocessing) model.

Details of the submission

By incorporating the preprocessing steps in a pipeline, different hyperparameters of the preprocessing can be tried out in a CV scheme. I wrote the following class to censor values in the dataset that lie outside ranges (specified as parameters to the transformer class):

which can then be passed through to a CV parameter search function (I used RandomizedSearchCV). Specifying a distribution for the pipeline parameter 'censor__good_range' will then optimize the pass-through variable range in the Censor transformer.

I was intending to specify ranges for all of the variables, but ended up just censoring the 'O2_1' column, which ended up with an optimized pass-through variable range of 4.5 to 14.3, which corresponds closely to the physically plausible range of this variable as discussed in the "overview" section above.

I also considered censoring based on frequency of data values under the assumption that missing values get coded as dummy values rather than NaN. To extend this approach I would include other variables for censoring, and have different ranges for each type of variable (which should be possible using the Censor class above). In the end I wasn't sure this approach would work due to the difficulty of getting anything better on the public leaderboard than just submitting the raw competition data.

### Data-centric approach. Why we need it. Quality of data is Paramount.

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s3e21/discussion/433516

Why we need data-centric AI

"Bad data costs the U.S. alone around $3 Trillion every year. Data quality issues plague almost every industry and dealing with them manually imposes an immense burden. As datasets grow larger, it becomes infeasible to ensure their quality without the use of algorithms ."

"Now more than ever, we need automated methods and systematic engineering principles to ensure ML models are being trained with clean data. As ML becomes intertwined with our daily lives in healthcare, finance, transportation, it is imperative these systems are trained in a reliable manner."

"Recent research is highlighting the value of data-centric AI for various applications. For image classification with noisily labeled data, a recent benchmark studied various methods to train models under increasing noise rates in the famous Cifar-10 dataset."

"The findings revealed that simple methods which adaptively change the dataset can lead to much more accurate models than methods which aim to account for the noise through sophisticated modeling strategies."

https://dcai.csail.mit.edu/lectures/data-centric-model-centric/

Andrew Ng Launches A Campaign For Data-Centric AI

By Gil Press - Jun 16, 2021

"In the dominant model-centric approach to AI, according to Ng, you collect all the data you can collect and develop a model good enough to deal with the noise in the data. The established process calls for holding the data fixed and iteratively improving the model until the desired results are achieved. In the nascent data-centric approach to AI, “consistency of data is paramount. To get to the right results, you hold the model or code fixed and iteratively improve the quality of the data."

Data is the most Undervalued and Deglamorised aspect of AI

"Paradoxically, data is the most undervalued and deglamorised aspect of AI” say Google researchers in a recent paper, reporting on their survey of 53 AI practitioners. They found that “data cascades compounding events causing negative, downstream effects from data issues triggered by conventional AI/ML practices that undervalue data quality… are pervasive (92% prevalence), invisible, delayed, but often avoidable.”

"As investments in AI projects spread from Internet-based, consumer-facing companies to other industries, the models are typically trained by 10,000 or less examples rather than millions of examples. That is a very good reason to pay greater attention to the quality of the data."

"Moreover, a hospital or a factory do not typically have on staff anyone with a machine learning expertise. What their employees have is data expertise, thorough knowledge of the domain in which they operate, even the specific organization where they work (there can be substantial differences in how hospitals collect, store, and manage their data, for example). Knowing how to assemble high-quality data in their domain is their professional competitive advantage."

A data-centric approach allows those without technical training in AI to feed it into an open source model

"A data-centric approach, allows people in manufacturing, hospitals, farms, to customize the data, making it more feasible for someone without technical training in AI to feed it into an open-source model. That will help uncover many new opportunities for AI to make an impact in traditional environments with small data sets and no AI expertise."

https://www.forbes.com/sites/gilpress/2021/06/16/andrew-ng-launches-a-campaign-for-data-centric-ai/?sh=6ca473a774f5
