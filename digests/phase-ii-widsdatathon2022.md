### Analytics Competition

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/phase-ii-widsdatathon2022/discussion/311833

You will notice a few things different about this Competition. This is the second phase to the WiDS Datathon. You do not make regular submissions and there is no leaderboard. Teams are allowed but you do not have to create teams as you would in a typical competition. You can form teams offline but make sure that all teammates are listed as collaborators when you submit. 

As a reminder your Submission must be completed by the deadline to be considered eligible for consideration.
