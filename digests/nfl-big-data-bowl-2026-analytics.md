Please remember that private sharing of competition code or data outside of your team is, as always, not permitted. Code sharing must always be done publicly through the Kaggle forums/notebooks.

Kaggle Staff and Hosts running competitions will not monitor Discord or be available to answer questions in Discord. This is intended to be a more casual space to discuss competitions and help each other. Please keep important questions, insights, writeups, and other valuable conversation on the Kaggle forums.

### 2025 Winner – AMA & Supplement Release

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/nfl-big-data-bowl-2026-analytics/discussion/614950

Hey everyone! 

My name's Vishakh and I was one of the winners for the NFL Big Data Bowl 2025! As a result of the competition, I was able to land a job in football at this company called SumerSports and am forever indebted to Dr. Mike Lopez, Tom Bliss, Ally Blake and the rest of the team at the NFL and Kaggle for putting this competition out there and letting people have a way to break into sports and just build cool stuff! 

I'm making this post for two reasons. 1) Part of the reason that me and my partner were able to succeed last year is because of the open-source resources that SumerSports and other individuals have put out into the community, and I want to pay it forward. With Sumer, I've been able to release a supplement of our production data, both frame level coverage scheme alongside some player level coverage details: 

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vishakhsandwar/sumersupplement-bdb-26
https://www.kaggle.com/code/vishakhsandwar/sumersports-supplement-investigation

Additionally, our work built off of Udit Ranasaria and Pavel Vabishchevich's open-source transformer model, I've fitted it again to this year's prompt to man vs zone coverage which was my topic last year.

https://www.kaggle.com/code/vishakhsandwar/modeling-with-transformers-2026

2) The second reason I'm making a discussion is because I know that this could be challenging for many outsiders who aren't too familiar with the sport or the competition, so if there's any questions I could answer or tips I could give, I'll be responsive to this discussion for the foreseeable future but for more personal topics or detailed discussion it's best to reach out to me on Twitter @VishakhS74! I've also detailed my personal thoughts and investigation in this blog post below:

https://sumersports.com/the-zone/sumersports-big-data-bowl-2026/

Good luck everyone and excited to see what you come up with!

### Welcome to the 2026 Big Data Bowl!

来源：https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/nfl-big-data-bowl-2026-analytics/discussion/609370

The National Football League is excited to be back with another Big Data Bowl. This year's competition focuses on player movement when the ball is in the air. We are hoping to use winning ideas and solutions to generate new stats both on offensive and defensive players, to get a better sense of both how players move on the field and how the passing game evolves from snap until the decision to throw.

As always, in addition to the goal of winning a top prize, please look at the Big Data Bowl as an opportunity to build your resume for careers in sports analytics. At this point, the pipeline of Big Data Bowl to sports analytics professional is extremely deep, and is a testament to the hard work of several participants in past. 

Don't hesitate to reach out for questions, and good luck!
